## 1. Purpose

This page introduces the university's services for **career orientation, internships and entering the job market**, and the rule on **residence after graduation**.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students — especially those with a compulsory internship or close to graduation.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Career Service (ZQS):** advice and workshops on career orientation, internships and starting work ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)).
2.  **Career Dates:** the university's job fair ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)).
3.  **Credit-bearing key-skills courses** and projects with companies ([ZQS – Key competencies](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc)). → [25. ZQS](../03-university-services/25-zqs.md)
4.  **University page on careers and internships:** [Career orientation & internships](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/praktikum-beruf).
5.  🌍 **Internships and the work limit:** a compulsory internship is part of your studies; for non-EU students a voluntary internship counts towards the limit or needs approval from the Federal Employment Agency. → [40. Student jobs](40-student-jobs.md)
6.  🌍 **After graduation:** non-EU graduates can get a residence permit to **look for work for up to 18 months**, provided their living costs are covered ([§ 20 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__20.html)). → [46. After graduation](46-after-graduation.md)

**Related pages:** [46. After graduation](46-after-graduation.md), [47. Leaving Germany](47-leaving-germany.md)
