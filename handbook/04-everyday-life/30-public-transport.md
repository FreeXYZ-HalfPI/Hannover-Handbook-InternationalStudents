## 1. Purpose

With the semester ticket (Deutschlandsemesterticket), paid with your semester fee, you can use almost all local and regional public transport in Germany at no extra cost. This page explains how to get the ticket, where it is valid and what is available for cyclists.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Validity:** all local and regional transport **throughout Germany** — buses, trams (Stadtbahn), S-Bahn and regional trains. **Long-distance trains (IC, ICE, EC) are not included** ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)).
2.  **On your phone:** log in to the **RIDE** portal with your Stud.IP login (Web-SSO) and download the ticket; it can be added to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)).
3.  **No smartphone:** send the chip-card request form with the subject "Chipkarte" to semesterticket@asta-hannover.de ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)).
4.  **Valid from:** 1 October (winter) and 1 April (summer) ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). Always carry ID (passport, ID card or residence card) with the ticket.
5.  **"Gesperrt" (blocked) shown during a ticket check:** open the ticket directly in the RIDE portal; the Wallet copy may be outdated. If you were fined, e-mail AStA with the subject "Bescheinigung Semesterticket" ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)).
6.  **Refunds or exemption:** for a stay of more than 120 consecutive days outside the ticket area, or a semester abroad or compulsory internship abroad, apply to AStA with proof (e.g. Learning Agreement) at the latest **one month after lectures start** ([AStA – Semester ticket hardship fund rules (PDF)](https://staff.asta-hannover.de/beta/assets/SemTicketAusfall_Ordnung.pdf); [AStA – Semester ticket (English)](https://www.asta-hannover.de/en/translate-to-english-semesterticket)). ⚠️ The refund form is no longer online; request it from mobilitaet@asta-hannover.de. A hardship fund exists for special cases — for example international students without a work permit or with a restricted one ([AStA – Hardship fund portal](https://staff.asta-hannover.de/)). ⚠️ These documents date from 2017–2021; ask mobilitaet@asta-hannover.de for current conditions.
7.  **Local transport in Hannover:** the GVH network (trams, buses and S-Bahn) — [uestra.de](https://www.uestra.de/).
8.  **Cycling:** Hannover is flat and bike-friendly. AStA runs a bike workshop (Fahrradwerkstatt), funded by a small part of the semester fee (€1.11) ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung); [AStA – Bike workshop](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/fahrradwerkstaette)). ℹ️ Use lights after dark and lock your bike well — bike theft is common. Cyclists must follow traffic rules; fines apply.
9.  **Re-activate every semester:** the ticket is issued anew each semester. From 1 October or 1 April log in to RIDE again and download the new ticket or add it to your Wallet ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). → [65. Annual student calendar](../07-resources/65-annual-calendar.md)
10. **Strikes and diversions:** ÜSTRA and regiobus were on strike several times in 2024–2026, and during construction weeks parts of some lines (e.g. 4 and 5 towards Garbsen) are replaced by buses (Ersatzverkehr). Before an exam or important appointment check [uestra.de](https://www.uestra.de/) or the ÜSTRA app. ℹ️ If there is a strike on an exam day, plan ahead and, if needed, e-mail your lecturer and the examinations office before the exam.

## 7. Student experiences (unofficial)

> [!NOTE]
> This section is for personal experiences; it is not a general rule or legal advice. Share yours (with a date) on [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md) or by opening a GitHub issue.

- **Strike days (2025–2026):** when only ÜSTRA or regiobus were on strike, the S-Bahn and regional trains (DB) usually still ran. Students got to university by bike, S-Bahn or sharing rides with friends.
- **Second-hand bikes and repairs:** the university bike sale and the free repair workshop (you only pay for parts) were recommended several times. → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)
