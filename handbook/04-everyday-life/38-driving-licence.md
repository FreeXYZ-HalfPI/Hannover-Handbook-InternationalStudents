## 1. Purpose

A foreign driving licence is valid in Germany for a limited time only — and the rules depend heavily on the country that issued it. This page explains how long you can drive with it and how to convert it into a German licence (Umschreibung).

## 2. Who needs this?

- 🌍 **EU/EEA licences** remain valid in Germany without conversion (until their expiry date) ([§ 28 FeV – EU/EEA licences](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__28.html)).
- 🌍 **Non-EU licences:** see the steps below. Whether you need driving tests to convert depends on the country (Annex 11 FeV).
- ℹ️ With the semester ticket you rarely need a car. → [30. Public transport](30-public-transport.md)

## 3. Steps

1.  **Validity of a non-EU licence:** valid for **six months** after you take up ordinary residence in Germany ([§ 29 FeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__29.html)).
2.  **Extension:** the licensing authority can extend this by up to six months if you prove you will not stay in Germany longer than twelve months ([§ 29 FeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__29.html)). ℹ️ For students staying several years this rarely applies — apply for conversion early.
3.  **Translation:** a licence not in German needs a translation from an internationally recognised automobile club of the issuing country or from a body designated by the German Transport Ministry — or an International Driving Permit ([§ 29 FeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__29.html)).
4.  🌍 **Which countries need no tests?** Annex 11 FeV lists countries whose class B licences are converted **without** theory and practical tests — among them Albania, Andorra, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Israel, Japan, Kosovo, Monaco, Montenegro, Namibia, New Zealand, North Macedonia, Serbia, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan, Ukraine, the United Kingdom, all Australian states and Canadian provinces, and many US states (some US states need the theory test only). Licences from countries **not** on the list (most countries in Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Middle East) need both a **theory and a practical test** ([Annex 11 FeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_11.html)). ⚠️ Check the current list for your country/state.
5.  **Conversion (city of Hannover):** apply **in person** with an **appointment** at the city's driving licence office (Fahrerlaubnisbehörde). Processing takes about three to four weeks ([hannover.gov.de – Conversion of third-country licences](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/fahrerlaubnis-umschreibung-einer-sonstigen-auslaendischen-fahrerlaubnis-drittstaaten--900000272-0.html?myMedium=1)).
6.  **If tests are needed:** you must register with a driving school, which registers you for the tests; lessons are not mandatory by law, but practical test preparation usually requires some. The theory test can be taken in several foreign languages, including English. ⚠️ Ask your driving school which languages are offered.
7.  **Residents of the Hannover region** (outside the city) apply at the Region Hannover licensing office ([hannover.de – Foreign driving licence](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/Mobilit%C3%A4t/Kraftfahrzeug-Stra%C3%9Fe/Fahrerlaubnis%C2%AD%C2%ADbeh%C3%B6rde-Region-Hannover/Ausl%C3%A4ndischer-F%C3%BChrerschein)).

- **Address:** Am Schützenplatz 1, 30169 Hannover
- **Contact:** city online appointments or 32.12Terminvergabe@Hannover-Stadt.de ([hannover.gov.de – Driving licence office](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/verwaltung/32-12-fahrerlaubnisbehoerde-900000753-30810.html))
