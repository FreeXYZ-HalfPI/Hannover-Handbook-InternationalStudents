## 1. Purpose

The **International Office (Hochschulbüro für Internationales)** and its **Welcome Centre** help international students with entry, residence, housing, orientation programmes and emergency financial aid ([LUH – International first-semester students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/internationale-erstsemesterstudierende)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **International students** of every nationality (EU and non-EU), exchange students, doctoral candidates and visiting researchers.

## 3. Steps

1.  **With or without an appointment:** come during office hours or book an in-person or online appointment by e-mail or phone ([LUH – International Office](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/praesidialstab-und-stabsstellen/internationales)).
2.  **Orientation programmes:** Orientation Days for international students, [Start Smart](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/start-smart) and [STEPin](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/preparation-courses-and-introductory-sessions/stepin) for Master's students ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/internationale-erstsemesterstudierende)). → [51. Events and networking](../06-student-community/51-events-networking.md)
3.  **Study-Buddy and Senior Student Partnership:** programmes that pair you with a student who already knows Hannover ([LUH – International students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende)). → [48. Programmes for international students at LUH](../06-student-community/48-international-programmes-luh.md)
4.  **Group appointment for address registration** → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)
5.  **Financial aid:** emergency aid (Notfall-Beihilfe) and degree-completion aid (Studienabschlussbeihilfe) for international students ([LUH – Financial aid for international students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)). → [43. Scholarships and funding](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
6.  **Refugees and students from crisis regions:** a dedicated contact advises refugees and prospective students with a refugee background ([LUH – Information for refugees](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/gefluechtete)). → [63. Refugees and people with protection status](../08-by-nationality/63-refugees-protection-status.md)
7.  **Events on immigration law** for studying and working are held regularly ([LUH – Events](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/aktuelles/veranstaltungen)).
8.  Follow the office on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/internationalofficeluh/) for current events and deadlines.
