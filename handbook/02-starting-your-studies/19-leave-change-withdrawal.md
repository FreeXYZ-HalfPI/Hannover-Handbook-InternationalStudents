## 1. Purpose

Sometimes studies don't go to plan: illness, a semester abroad, changing interests or deciding to stop. This page explains the three official options: leave of absence (Beurlaubung), change of programme (Studiengangwechsel) and de-registration (Exmatrikulation).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students facing problems or changes — especially non-EU students.

## 3. Steps

**Leave of absence (Beurlaubung)**

1.  **A reason is required:** e.g. illness, a semester abroad, pregnancy or other personal reasons, with proof (e.g. a medical certificate) ([LUH – Leave of absence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/studienorganisation/studierendenstatus/beurlaubung)).
2.  **When to apply:** at re-registration (Rückmeldung) and at the latest by the last day of the lecture period; in exceptional cases up to one month after the semester starts. Send the "Beurlaubungsbogen" form and proof as one PDF to Studierendenverwaltung@uni-hannover.de ([LUH – Leave of absence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/studienorganisation/studierendenstatus/beurlaubung)).
3.  **Limits:** usually at most **two consecutive semesters** per programme and at most **four semesters** in total; up to six for childcare. Leave is only for full semesters and usually **not possible in your first semester** (exception: a semester abroad in an integrated Master's with written approval of the faculty's dean of studies) ([LUH Enrolment Regulations – § 9 (PDF)](https://www.uni-hannover.de/fileadmin/luh/content/i-amt/antraege/gesetze/Immatrikulationsordnung_der_Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Universitaet_Hannover.pdf)).
4.  **Effects:** during leave you cannot attend courses or take exams; the leave semester counts as a university semester but not as a subject semester. **The semester fee still has to be paid**, unless the fee regulations say otherwise ([LUH Enrolment Regulations – § 9](https://www.uni-hannover.de/fileadmin/luh/content/i-amt/antraege/gesetze/Immatrikulationsordnung_der_Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Universitaet_Hannover.pdf); [LUH – Leave of absence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/studienorganisation/studierendenstatus/beurlaubung)). → [13. Semester fee](13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
5.  🌍 **Residence permit:** a leave semester (e.g. for illness) is normally compatible with a student permit, but you may not be allowed to work full-time during it. ⚠️ Inform the Ausländerbehörde.

**Change of programme (Studiengangwechsel)**

1.  You must **apply again within the normal deadlines** for the new programme; you remain enrolled in the old one until admitted to the new one ([LUH – Change of programme](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/studiengangs-und-fachwechsel)).
2.  🌍 **Residence:** a study permit is issued for a specific programme, and the Ausländerbehörde must be told about a change. § 16b AufenthG does not regulate a change of programme explicitly; it only says the permit is extended if the study purpose can still be achieved within a reasonable time ([§ 16b (2) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html)). ⚠️ Ask the Ausländerbehörde before you switch. → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)

**De-registration (Exmatrikulation)**

1.  You can apply in writing at any time; send the signed form to Studierendenverwaltung@uni-hannover.de. De-registration is not retroactive; if you apply within one month of the start of lectures, the semester fee is refunded ([LUH – De-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/studienorganisation/studierendenstatus/exmatrikulation)).
2.  🌍 **Residence after withdrawal:** a study permit depends on enrolment; talk to the Ausländerbehörde before de-registering. Options may include vocational training, a job or a new programme. → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md), [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)
3.  **Struggling at the start?** The university's page on difficult study starts: [Studien(fehl)start](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/studienfehlstart). Advice → [29. Counselling](../03-university-services/29-counselling.md)
