# 7. Health Insurance — Krankenversicherung

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 1 — Arriving in Hannover & First Steps](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

You cannot enrol at the university without health insurance, and proof of insurance is also needed for the residence permit ([Studentenwerk Hannover – First steps](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.
- 🌍 **EU/EEA and Swiss students** with statutory insurance at home can use the **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)**; the German insurer then confirms the exemption to the university. For longer stays German insurance can still be useful ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
- 🌍 **Countries with a social security agreement:** students insured at home in some non-EU countries (e.g. Türkiye, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Tunisia) may be able to use their home insurance with a certificate from their insurer. Check with the German statutory insurer before you arrive ([DVKA – Social security agreements](https://www.dvka.de/en/)). ⚠️ Coverage depends on the specific agreement.
- 🌍 **All other non-EU students** need insurance for their whole stay; enrolled full-time students can join statutory insurance at the student rate ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
- The student rate in statutory insurance is generally available until the end of the semester in which you turn 30 ([gesetzlichekrankenkassen.de](https://www.gesetzlichekrankenkassen.de/kassenbeitraege/studenten) — unofficial source).

## 3. Steps

1.  Choose a statutory health insurer (gesetzliche Krankenkasse). Studentenwerk names these insurers with offices in Hannover: AOK, BARMER, DAK, KKH and TK ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)). All statutory insurers cover essentially the same benefits by law; they differ mainly in the additional contribution and service (e.g. English-language support).
2.  Ask the insurer to send the **electronic insurance notification (M10)** directly to the university ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany); [LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)).
3.  **Private insurance or exemption:** if you choose private insurance, you must apply to a statutory insurer for exemption within three months of enrolment, and the exemption is **irrevocable for your whole studies** ([§ 8 SGB V](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_5/__8.html)). The private policy must meet the requirements of § 11 (1)–(3) SGB V for the residence permit ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). ℹ️ Over 30? Statutory student rates end; compare voluntary statutory and private options carefully.
4.  Enrolled students are automatically covered by **statutory accident insurance** for university activities ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
5.  After joining, always carry your insurance card and show it at the doctor and pharmacy. → [33. Doctors and pharmacies](../04-everyday-life/33-doctor-pharmacy.md)

## 5. Responsible office

- **Official name:** your chosen health insurer; each insurer's Hannover offices are on its website ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).
- **Insurance information at the university:** [LUH – Health insurance](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/krankenversicherung)
- **Social security agreements:** Deutsche Verbindungsstelle Krankenversicherung – Ausland (DVKA) — [dvka.de](https://www.dvka.de/en/)
