# 59. EU, EEA and Swiss Citizens

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 8 — Information by Nationality](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

As an EU/EEA or Swiss citizen you enjoy freedom of movement, so many steps in this handbook do not apply to you. This page summarises what you **do** and **don't** need.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Citizens of the 27 EU countries and of Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway (EEA).
- Swiss citizens (similar rules with a few differences).
- Non-EU family members of EU citizens.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Entry:** a valid passport or national ID card is enough; no visa ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
2.  **Stays over 90 days:** you must have sufficient health insurance and enough money to live on, but you do not need to prove this in advance ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt); [Freedom of Movement Act § 4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/freiz_gg_eu_2004/__4.html)). No blocked account is required.
3.  **Register your address** like everyone else, within two weeks of moving in. → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md) ℹ️ With an EU ID card that has an online function, you may be able to register online.
4.  **Residence permit:** EU/EEA citizens need none. **Swiss citizens** must apply for a residence permit (Aufenthaltserlaubnis-CH) at the Ausländerbehörde for stays over 90 days ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
5.  **Health insurance:** if you have statutory insurance at home, bring your **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)**. A German statutory insurer confirms to the university that you are exempt from German student insurance. If you work more than a minijob in Germany, you usually must switch to German insurance ([LUH – Health insurance](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/krankenversicherung); [Your Europe – Health cover for students](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/health/unplanned-healthcare/temporary-stays/index_en.htm)). → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)
6.  **Application:** EU applicants often have later deadlines (e.g. 15 July / 15 January for many Master's programmes) and may apply directly instead of via uni-assist — check your programme ([LUH – Application & admission](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung)). → [1. Applying](../01-arriving-in-hannover/01-application-uni-assist.md)
7.  **Work:** no residence-law limits; social-insurance rules for students apply as for Germans. → [40. Student jobs](../05-work-finance-career/40-student-jobs.md), [42. Tax](../05-work-finance-career/42-tax-payslip.md)
8.  **BAföG:** EU citizens may be eligible after five years of residence in Germany, or earlier in some cases (e.g. if you or your parents have worked in Germany) ([§ 8 BAföG – eligible foreign nationals](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/baf_g/__8.html)). → [43. Scholarships](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
9.  **Driving licence:** EU/EEA licences remain valid; no conversion needed ([§ 28 FeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__28.html)). → [38. Driving licence](../04-everyday-life/38-driving-licence.md)
10. **Phone and banking:** EU roaming and SEPA rules apply; you may keep your home account, but landlords and employers often prefer a German IBAN. → [6. Bank account](../01-arriving-in-hannover/06-bank-account.md), [8. SIM card](../01-arriving-in-hannover/08-sim-internet.md)
11. **Voting:** EU citizens living in Germany can vote in local (municipal) and European Parliament elections ([Federal Returning Officer – EU citizens](https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de/en/europawahlen/2024/informationen-waehler/unionsbuerger.html)).
12. **Erasmus+:** exchange students should contact the exchange coordinators ([LUH – Exchange students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/exchange-students)).
