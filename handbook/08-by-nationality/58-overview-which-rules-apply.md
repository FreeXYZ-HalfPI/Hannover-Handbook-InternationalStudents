## 1. Purpose

Most of this handbook applies to every international student. But visa, residence, work, insurance and recognition rules differ a lot depending on **where you come from** and **what status you have**. This page tells you which group you belong to and which pages to read.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **Every** international student — read it before anything else.

## 3. Find your group

| Group | Who | Visa before arrival | Residence permit | Work limit | Read next |
|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| **A — EU / EEA** | Citizens of EU countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway | No | No (just register your address) | None | [59](59-eu-eea-swiss.md) |
| **B — Switzerland** | Swiss citizens | No | Yes, free-movement permit for stays over 90 days | None | [59](59-eu-eea-swiss.md) |
| **C — Visa-free for study and work** | Australia, Canada, Israel, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, United Kingdom, USA | No | Yes, apply in Germany within 90 days | 140 days/year (study permit) | [60](60-visa-free-nationalities.md) |
| **D — Visa-free for study only** | Andorra, Brazil, El Salvador, Honduras, Monaco, San Marino | No (if you won't work before getting the permit) | Yes, apply in Germany within 90 days | 140 days/year (study permit) | [60](60-visa-free-nationalities.md) |
| **E — Visa required** | All other countries | **Yes — national visa (type D)** | Yes, before the visa expires | 140 days/year | [61](61-visa-required-countries-aps.md) |
| **E+ — APS countries** | School/university education in China, India or Vietnam | Yes, plus **APS certificate** | Yes | 140 days/year | [61](61-visa-required-countries-aps.md) |
| **F — EU mobility** | Non-EU students already holding a study permit from another EU country (exchange/joint programmes) | No (up to 360 days via notification) | Not for up to 360 days | Rules of the mobility scheme | [10](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md) |
| **G — Refugees and protection status** | Asylum seekers, recognised refugees, people with subsidiary or temporary protection (e.g. Ukraine) | — | Your existing status | Depends on status | [63](63-refugees-protection-status.md) |

Sources: [LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt); [§ 41 AufenthV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthv/__41.html); [§ 16b AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html); [Federal Foreign Office – Visa requirements overview](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/-/231148).
