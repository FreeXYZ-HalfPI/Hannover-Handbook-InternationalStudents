# 9. Tax ID (Steuer-ID) and Official Letters

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 1 — Arriving in Hannover & First Steps](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

After registering your address, two important letters usually arrive: your **tax identification number (Steuerliche Identifikationsnummer, Steuer-ID)**, needed for any job, and a letter about the **broadcasting fee (Rundfunkbeitrag)**. This page explains what to do with each.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **Steuer-ID:** everyone registering an address in Germany for the first time — especially anyone with a student job. Nationality does not matter.
- **Rundfunkbeitrag:** paid once per dwelling (Wohnung), not per person.

## 3. Steps

**Tax ID (Steuer-ID)**

1.  After your first registration, the registration office passes your data to the **Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt)**, which sends your number **automatically** by post to your registered address — you do not need to apply ([BZSt – Tax identification number](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Privatpersonen/SteuerlicheIdentifikationsnummer/steuerlicheidentifikationsnummer_node.html)).
2.  According to the university, the letter arrives in about two to three weeks ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). ℹ️ Put your full name on your letterbox so the post can be delivered.
3.  If it has not arrived **three months** after registration, send a copy of your ID and your registration certificate to the BZSt ([BZSt – FAQ](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Privatpersonen/SteuerlicheIdentifikationsnummer/FAQ/faq_node.html)). The number is only sent by post to the registered address.
4.  Keep the letter; employers and sometimes banks ask for it ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). → [40. Student jobs](../05-work-finance-career/40-student-jobs.md)

**Broadcasting fee (Rundfunkbeitrag)**

1.  The fee is **€18.36 per month per dwelling**, not per person ([Rundfunkbeitrag – Students](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/buergerinnen-und-buerger/informationen/informationen-fuer-studierende)).
2.  **Shared flat (WG):** only one adult resident must register and pay; residents decide among themselves who ([Rundfunkbeitrag – Students](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/buergerinnen-und-buerger/informationen/informationen-fuer-studierende)). Others register as "already covered" with the payer's contribution number.
3.  **Halls of residence:** a room entered from a common corridor counts as its own dwelling and pays separately — even with a shared bathroom or kitchen ([Rundfunkbeitrag – Students](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/buergerinnen-und-buerger/informationen/informationen-fuer-studierende)).
4.  **Exemption:** BAföG recipients not living with their parents can apply for exemption ([betanet](https://www.betanet.de/rundfunkbeitrag-befreiung-ermaessigung.html) — unofficial source). 🌍 BAföG usually does not cover international students, with exceptions (e.g. EU citizens with long residence, refugees) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [43. Scholarships and funding](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
5.  English information: [Rundfunkbeitrag – Students and apprentices](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/welcome/english/students-and-apprentices).
6.  **Do not ignore it:** unpaid fees are collected with reminders and can end in enforcement. Register (or deregister when you leave) online. ℹ️

## 5. Responsible office

- **Steuer-ID:** Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt), Federal Central Tax Office — postal address: Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, Referat St II 7, 11055 Berlin ([BZSt – FAQ](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Privatpersonen/SteuerlicheIdentifikationsnummer/FAQ/faq_node.html))
- **Rundfunkbeitrag:** ARD ZDF Deutschlandradio Beitragsservice — [rundfunkbeitrag.de](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/)
