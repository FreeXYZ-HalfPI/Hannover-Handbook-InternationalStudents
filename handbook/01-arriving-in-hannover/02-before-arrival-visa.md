# 2. Before You Arrive — Visa, Proof of Finances and Preparation

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 1 — Arriving in Hannover & First Steps](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

This page lists what must be done **before you travel** to Hannover: visa, proof of financial resources, insurance, accommodation and enrolment documents. Mistakes or delays at this stage are hard to fix later.

## 2. Who needs this?

- 🌍 **What you need depends on your citizenship:**

| Your citizenship | Visa before travelling? | Where to read more |
|----|----|----|
| EU / EEA | No — ID card or passport is enough | [59. EU, EEA and Swiss citizens](../08-by-nationality/59-eu-eea-swiss.md) |
| Switzerland | No visa; residence permit after arrival | [59. EU, EEA and Swiss citizens](../08-by-nationality/59-eu-eea-swiss.md) |
| Australia, Canada, Israel, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, United Kingdom, USA (and for study only: Andorra, Brazil, El Salvador, Honduras, Monaco, San Marino) | No — residence permit applied for in Germany within 90 days | [60. Visa-free nationalities](../08-by-nationality/60-visa-free-nationalities.md) |
| All other countries | **Yes — national visa (type D)** | [61. Visa-required countries & APS](../08-by-nationality/61-visa-required-countries-aps.md) |

- **All students:** accommodation, health insurance and enrolment documents.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Get a national visa (type D), not a Schengen visa (type C).** Only a type D visa can be converted into a residence permit for stays over 90 days; ask for multiple entry ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). A tourist visa does not allow you to enrol and cannot be converted.
2.  If you do not yet have an unconditional admission, or must first attend a language course or Studienkolleg, there is a **visa for prospective students** (residence for up to 9 months to find a study place) ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
3.  **Find your German mission** (embassy or consulate) and its rules. Waiting times for appointments vary from a few days to many months depending on the country, and many missions use an external service provider (e.g. VFS Global or TLScontact) or the online **Consular Services Portal**. Apply only to the mission responsible for your place of residence ([Federal Foreign Office – German missions abroad](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/ReiseUndSicherheit/deutsche-auslandsvertretungen/03-webseitenav); [Federal Foreign Office – Visa Navigator](https://digital.diplo.de/navigator/en/visa); [Consular Services Portal](https://digital.diplo.de/visa)). 🌍 Book your appointment as soon as you have your admission letter — or earlier if your mission runs a waiting list.
4.  **Proof of financial resources.** Most students use a **blocked account (Sperrkonto)**; alternatives are a scholarship or a formal obligation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung) from someone living in Germany. Only an official confirmation showing the total amount and the monthly payout is accepted ([Federal Foreign Office (German Embassy Managua) – Blocked account](https://managua.diplo.de/ni-de/service/05-visaeinreise/sperrkonto-2163974)). For 2026 the German missions ask for **€992 per month, i.e. €11,904 per year** for regular study ([German missions in Italy – Visa news](https://italien.diplo.de/it-de/service/visa-aktuell/2586756-2586756)). Applicants for the 9‑month prospective-student residence may be asked for more (one mission lists €1,091/month) ([German Embassy San José – Merkblatt Studium (PDF)](https://san-jose.diplo.de/resource/blob/1540562/fddac503242f24102b78bf7523dd7aba/merkblatt-studium-data.pdf)). ⚠️ The amount is reviewed regularly — confirm it on your mission's website before transferring money.
5.  **Health insurance.** Non-EU students need insurance for their whole stay (usually travel/incoming insurance for the first days). After enrolment, students can join statutory health insurance at the student rate ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). → [7. Health insurance](07-health-insurance.md)
6.  **Accommodation:** start searching as early as possible. → [4. Finding accommodation](04-housing.md)
7.  **Personal liability insurance (Haftpflichtversicherung)** is strongly recommended ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
8.  Prepare your **enrolment documents** (originals, translations, admission letter). → [12. Admission and enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md)
9.  Use the personal roadmap **[EasyStartLUH](https://go.lu-h.de/EasyStartLUH)** ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
10. **Bring with you:** passport (valid well beyond your first year), originals and certified copies of your certificates, biometric photos, an international or translated driving licence if you plan to drive, prescriptions for regular medication (in English), and enough money (or a card that works abroad) for the first weeks — rent deposit and first rent are often due before your blocked account pays out. ℹ️ General advice; see [3. Arrival](03-arrival-transport.md).

## 4. Documents and costs

| Item | Required / possible | Notes | Source |
|----|----|----|----|
| National visa (type D) | Required (visa-required nationalities) | Visa fee usually €75 | [LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt) |
| Admission letter | Required | Stating programme and start semester | [LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt) |
| Proof of finances (blocked account, scholarship or Verpflichtungserklärung) | Required (non-EU) | €992/month = €11,904/year in 2026 | [German missions in Italy](https://italien.diplo.de/it-de/service/visa-aktuell/2586756-2586756) |
| Scholarship below the monthly amount | Possible | The gap must be covered, e.g. with a blocked account | [Federal Foreign Office](https://managua.diplo.de/ni-de/service/05-visaeinreise/sperrkonto-2163974) |
| APS certificate | Required (China, India, Vietnam) | Before the visa application | [61. Visa-required countries & APS](../08-by-nationality/61-visa-required-countries-aps.md) |
| Travel or health insurance | Required | For the whole stay | [LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt) |
| Personal liability insurance | Possible (recommended) | — | [LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt) |

> [!WARNING]
> Blocked-account providers are private companies. The Federal Foreign Office does not recommend any provider. Compare fees and check that your mission accepts the provider before paying. If your country is affected by sanctions or banking restrictions, read [62. Sanctions and payment restrictions](../08-by-nationality/62-sanctions-payment-restrictions.md) first.

- **University:** Hochschulbüro für Internationales – Welcome Centre (International Office), Welfengarten 1A, 30167 Hannover, building 1106, room 034 · incoming-student@uni-hannover.de · +49 511 762 188 99 ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/internationale-erstsemesterstudierende))
