## 1. Purpose

Ways to volunteer at the university and in the city — to help others, practise German, build a network and strengthen your CV.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Become a Study-Buddy** and help new international students ([Study-Buddy](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende/betreuungsangebote/study-buddy)).
2.  **Join student groups or AStA** ([Student associations](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/studentische-vereinigungen)). → [49. International student groups](49-international-groups.md)
3.  **Get involved in your Fachschaft.** → [20. Fachschaft](../02-starting-your-studies/20-fachschaft.md)
4.  **A-Team:** student-to-student contact point on discrimination ([ChancenVielfalt](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/de/beratung)). → [27. ChancenVielfalt](../03-university-services/27-chancenvielfalt.md)
5.  **Help maintain this handbook** — fix information or add your experience. → [57. Suggestions and experiences](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md)
6.  **Sustainability projects** in the ZQS key-skills programme, with ECTS credits ([ZQS – Key competencies](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc)).
7.  **Volunteering in Hannover:** the city's volunteer phone line run by Freiwilligenzentrum Hannover: **0800 330 20 30** (free; Mon 10:00–18:00, Wed and Thu 14:00–18:00) · the city's volunteer network HannoNetz: [hannonetz.de](https://www.hannonetz.de/) · 0511 168-41825 ([hannover.de – Volunteer phone line](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/Politik/B%C3%BCrgerbeteiligung-Engagement/Ehrenamt/Wertsch%C3%A4tzung-und-Anerkennung/Freiwilligentelefon)).
