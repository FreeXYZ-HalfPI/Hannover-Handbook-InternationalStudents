## 1. Purpose

CVs and applications in Germany follow their own format and conventions. This page shows where to look for jobs and where to get help writing your application.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students looking for a student job, internship or first job after graduation.

## 3. Steps

1.  **The university Career Service** helps students of all subjects with job search, applications, internships and contacts with employers ([ZQS – Career Service](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc/career-service)). → [45. Career service](45-career-service.md)
2.  **Individual advice** on CVs and applications: [Individual advice](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc/career-guidance-and-service/individual-advice).
3.  **Programme for international students** at the Career Service: [Internationals welcome – Students](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc/career-service/internationals-welcome/students).
4.  **Career Dates job fair** ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)).
5.  **Career Service office hour at the Infothek:** Mondays 13:00–14:00 ([ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
6.  **Job portals:** the Federal Employment Agency's job exchange ([BA – Jobsuche](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/)) and the official portal for international professionals ([Make it in Germany – Job listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-listings)).
7.  **German CV (Lebenslauf):** usually tabular and in reverse chronological order, one to two pages, sent with a cover letter (Anschreiben) and relevant certificates. A photo and date of birth are common but not required — employers may not discriminate on such grounds (General Equal Treatment Act). ℹ️ Sample templates: [Europass CV](https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv).
8.  🌍 **Foreign degrees and professions:** for regulated professions (e.g. teaching, medicine, some engineering titles) you need formal recognition; the "Recognition Finder" tells you what applies ([Recognition in Germany – Recognition Finder](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/index.php)).
### Where to find job ads

9.  **jobicco (LUH):** short-term and part-time student jobs such as tutoring, retail and events ([jobicco Uni Hannover](https://jobicco.uni-hannover.de/)).
10. **Stellenticket (LUH):** internships, working-student (Werkstudent) jobs, theses in companies, trainee programmes, graduate jobs and research assistant positions, run with the Career Service ([Stellenticket LUH](https://stellenticket.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
11. **Federal Employment Agency job search:** filter by city, radius, field and working hours (full-time, part-time, Minijob) ([BA – Jobsuche](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/)).
12. **Research assistant (HiWi) jobs** at university institutes → [40. Student jobs](40-student-jobs.md)

### Free help

13. **Career guidance at the Hannover employment agency (Berufsberatung für Studierende):** help choosing a career path, labour-market outlook, choosing a master's, and doubts about continuing your studies — **free and confidential**. Book online; drop-in hour for study doubts on Thursdays 12:00–14:00 at Escherstraße 17 ([Agentur für Arbeit Hannover – Studies](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/hannover/studium)).
14. **Recognition of foreign qualifications for work (IQ):** IHK Hannover offers **free advice** on recognition and adaptation training for all occupations in the "Integration durch Qualifizierung" programme ([IHK Hannover – Recognition of foreign qualifications](https://www.ihk.de/hannover/hauptnavigation/ausbildung-und-weiterbildung/fachkraefte/anerkennung-auslaendischer-abschluesse2)). For evaluating a university degree → [66. Documents and credential evaluation](../07-resources/66-documents-translations-certified-copies.md)
15. **Employment-law questions:** AStA's advice for international students → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)

### Managing your applications

16. **Keep a tracking table:** company, job title, date sent, contact person, status (sent / interview / rejected) and follow-up date. ℹ️ Students found this prevents forgetting and double applications.
17. **Tailor each application:** a short cover letter that answers the requirements of that ad; send all documents as one PDF. ℹ️
18. **Follow up:** if you hear nothing after about two weeks, send a short, polite e-mail. ℹ️
19. **Before signing:** check the working-hour limit of your student residence permit; if the contract runs until your permit expires, ask the employer for a letter stating that the contract will be extended if your permit is. → [40](40-student-jobs.md), [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)

## 7. Student experiences (unofficial)

> [!NOTE]
> This section is for personal experiences; it is not a general rule or legal advice. Share yours (with a date) on [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md) or by opening a GitHub issue.

- **General job or one in your field?** Students tried both: general jobs (bakery, cashier, warehouse, phone support) are found faster, while Werkstudent jobs help your career. Common advice: start applying for Werkstudent jobs early in your studies — you don't need perfect German or full skills.
- **Apply every day:** several students filtered job portals by "last 24 hours" and "part-time", sent a few applications every day, and got answers.
- **German:** for general jobs, even basic German greatly improved the chances. → [35. Learning German](../04-everyday-life/35-learning-german.md)
- **Pension insurance number:** HiWi contracts ask for it; it is on the letter from your insurer or on a previous payslip.
