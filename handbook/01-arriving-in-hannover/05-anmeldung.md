# 5. Registering Your Address — Anmeldung / Bürgeramt

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-26 · **Section:** [Section 1 — Arriving in Hannover & First Steps](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

Everyone who moves into a flat or room in Germany must officially register their address (Anmeldung). Without the registration certificate (Meldebescheinigung) the next steps — bank account, tax ID, library card and residence permit — are not possible ([Studentenwerk Hannover – First steps](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany); [LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students living in the city or region of Hannover — **regardless of nationality**, including EU citizens.
- You must register again every time you move ([Studentenwerk Hannover](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Deadline:** within **two weeks** of moving in ([hannover.gov.de – Registering a flat](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/anmeldung-einer-wohnung-zuzug-nach-hannover-900000016-0.html?myMedium=1)).
2.  **Get the landlord's confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung)** from your landlord or main tenant, printed and signed. **A rental contract is not accepted instead** ([hannover.gov.de](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/anmeldung-einer-wohnung-zuzug-nach-hannover-900000016-0.html?myMedium=1)). Official city form: [PDF](https://hannover.gov.de/medien/dokumente/wohnungsgeberbestaetigung.pdf).
3.  **Book an appointment.** All citizens' offices (Bürgerämter) in the city of Hannover work by appointment only, and city residents may go to any of them ([hannover.de – Bürgeramt Aegi](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/B%C3%BCrger-Service/B%C3%BCrger-Service-in-der-Landeshauptstadt-Hannover/B%C3%BCrger%C3%A4mter-der-Landeshauptstadt/B%C3%BCrger%C3%A4mter-in-Hannover/B%C3%BCrgeramt-Aegi-ehemals-Mitte)). Booking: [termin.hannover.gov.de/buergeramt](https://termin.hannover.gov.de/buergeramt). ℹ️ New slots are often released early in the morning; check daily.
4.  **University group appointment:** each semester the International Office arranges a group appointment for new international students at Bürgeramt Aegidientorplatz; a request does not guarantee a place ([LUH – Appointment request Bürgeramt](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/formularsammlung/formularsammlung-hi/date-request-registration-with-the-city-of-hannover-buergeramt)). ℹ️ For winter 2026/27 the request deadline was 1 October and the group appointment 13 October 2026; check the same page for the next semester.
5.  **Online registration** has been possible since 2025, but currently not for holders of an electronic residence permit (eAT) ([hannover.gov.de – Electronic registration](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/online/angebot/elektronische-wohnsitzanmeldung-1131-30810.html)). 🌍 It needs an ID card or eAT with online function — most first-time arrivals from outside the EU therefore register in person.
6.  If you live in the **Hannover region** outside the city (e.g. Garbsen), go to that town's Bürgeramt or Einwohnermeldeamt ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/formularsammlung/formularsammlung-hi/date-request-registration-with-the-city-of-hannover-buergeramt); [Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).
7.  **Religion** on the form is optional, but depending on what you declare, **church tax (Kirchensteuer)** may later be deducted from your salary ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
8.  **Name spelling:** make sure your name is written exactly as in your passport — this avoids problems later with the bank, tax office and residence permit. ℹ️ General advice.
9.  Keep the certificate; you need it for the bank and the TIB library card ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [6. Bank account](06-bank-account.md)
10. **Your landlord has a duty too:** the landlord (Wohnungsgeber) must confirm your move-in in writing or electronically within two weeks ([§ 19 BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)). Not issuing the confirmation, or not registering on time, is an offence with a fine of up to €1,000 ([§ 54 BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__54.html)). → [4. Finding accommodation](04-housing.md)
