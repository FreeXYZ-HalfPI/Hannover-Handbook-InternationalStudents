## 1. Purpose

How to get from the airport or station to your accommodation and the university, and which ticket to use in your first days.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** newcomers — especially those who arrive before the semester starts, i.e. before the semester ticket is valid.

## 3. Steps

1.  **From Hannover Airport (HAJ):** the **S5** suburban train runs every 30 minutes and takes about 17–20 minutes to Hannover Hauptbahnhof (central station); the station is below Terminal C ([Hannover Airport – Transport & Directions](https://www.hannover-airport.de/en/traffic-connection/transport-directions/)).
2.  **Fare zone:** the airport is in **zone B** of the GVH network ([ÜSTRA – Tickets and subscriptions](https://www.uestra.de/en/tickets-prices/tickets-and-subscriptions/)). ⚠️ Check the current single-ticket price on the ÜSTRA site or at the ticket machine; ÜSTRA service line +49 511 1668-0.
3.  **Arriving via Frankfurt, Hamburg, Berlin or another hub?** Long-distance ICE/IC trains reach Hannover Hbf directly; book with Deutsche Bahn ([Deutsche Bahn](https://int.bahn.de/en)). ℹ️ Saver fares are cheaper when booked early.
4.  **The semester ticket (Deutschlandsemesterticket)** becomes valid on the first day of the semester: 1 October (winter) or 1 April (summer) ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). If you arrive earlier, buy a separate ticket. → [30. Public transport](../04-everyday-life/30-public-transport.md)
5.  **University:** the main building (Welfenschloss) is at Welfengarten 1, 30167 Hannover ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)). Directions: [LUH – How to get here](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/wegweiser/anfahrtsbeschreibung). → [14. Campus and buildings](../02-starting-your-studies/14-campus-buildings.md)
6.  Find buildings with the **[Standortfinder](https://standortfinder.uni-hannover.de/)** ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
7.  **No room yet?** Studentenwerk lists temporary options such as Jugendherberge Hannover (youth hostel), Naturfreundehaus and Bed'nBudget ([Studentenwerk – Housing search](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/wohnungssuche)). → [4. Finding accommodation](04-housing.md)
8.  **First-week checklist:** register your address, open a bank account, finish enrolment and health insurance. → [54. Checklist](../07-resources/54-checklist.md)
9.  **Cash at the airport:** if you arrive from a country outside the EU carrying **€10,000 or more** in cash (or equivalent), you must **declare it in writing** to customs. There is no upper limit, and the declaration is free ([Zoll – Cash](https://www.zoll.de/EN/Private-individuals/Travel/Entering-Germany/Restrictions/Cash/cash_node.html)). ℹ️ Keep the customs receipt — banks usually ask where large cash deposits come from. → [6. Bank account](06-bank-account.md)

## 5. Responsible office

- **Public transport:** Großraum-Verkehr Hannover (GVH) / ÜSTRA — [uestra.de](https://www.uestra.de/); service line +49 511 1668-0
- **Semester ticket:** AStA Hannover, Welfengarten 2c, 30167 Hannover · semesterticket@asta-hannover.de ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket))
