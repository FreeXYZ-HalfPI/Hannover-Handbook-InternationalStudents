## 3. Questions and answers

**What should I do first?** Register your address (Anmeldung) — the bank, Steuer-ID, library and residence permit all depend on it ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)). → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)

**I don't have a permanent home yet — can I register?** Yes, a temporary address works if you have a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung; register again after moving ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany); [hannover.gov.de](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/anmeldung-einer-wohnung-zuzug-nach-hannover-900000016-0.html?myMedium=1)). → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)

**🌍 Do I need a visa?** EU/EEA and Swiss citizens: no. Citizens of Australia, Canada, Israel, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, the UK and the USA (and, for study only, Andorra, Brazil, El Salvador, Honduras, Monaco and San Marino) can enter without a visa and apply for a residence permit in Germany. Everyone else needs a national type D visa ([§ 41 AufenthV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthv/__41.html); [LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). → [58. Which rules apply to you?](../08-by-nationality/58-overview-which-rules-apply.md)

**How much money do I need to show?** For 2026, €992 per month (€11,904 per year), usually in a blocked account ([German missions in Italy](https://italien.diplo.de/it-de/service/visa-aktuell/2586756-2586756)). → [2. Before you arrive](../01-arriving-in-hannover/02-before-arrival-visa.md)

**How much is the semester fee?** €453.16 for winter 2026/27, including the semester ticket ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)). → [13. Semester fee](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)

**Are there tuition fees?** No, apart from the semester fee — for both EU and non-EU students. Only if your "study credit" runs out (usually after 12 semesters for a Bachelor's) is a long-term fee of €500 per semester charged ([LUH – Study costs](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/studienkosten)). → [13. Semester fee](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)

**How much is health insurance per month?** About €146–151 for statutory student insurance in 2026 ([BARMER](https://www.barmer.de/unsere-leistungen/beitraege-tarife/krankenversicherung-studierende)). 🌍 EU students with an EHIC may be exempt. → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)

**How much can I work?** 🌍 Non-EU students: 140 working days per year (half days and weekly counting possible); student-assistant jobs at the university don't count. EU students: no residence-law limit ([§ 16b AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html)). → [40. Student jobs](../05-work-finance-career/40-student-jobs.md)

**My permit is expiring and there is no decision on my extension yet.** If you applied before it expired, your previous permit stays valid until the decision and you receive a Fiktionsbescheinigung ([§ 81 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__81.html)). → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)

**Can I get BAföG?** Usually not, with exceptions for some EU citizens, refugees and long-term residents ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). Other aid → [43. Scholarships](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)

**Where is the semester ticket valid?** On local and regional transport throughout Germany; IC and ICE trains are not included ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). → [30. Public transport](../04-everyday-life/30-public-transport.md)

**I forgot my LUH-ID password.** Go to the Infothek in the ServiceCenter ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [17. Online systems](../02-starting-your-studies/17-online-systems.md)

**I'm not feeling well and feel lonely.** Free, confidential counselling at ptb ([ptb](https://www.ptb.uni-hannover.de/en/)); in an emergency call 112. → [29. Counselling](../03-university-services/29-counselling.md)

**Can I drive with my foreign licence?** 🌍 EU licences: yes. Non-EU licences: for six months after taking up residence, with a translation; after that you must convert it — with or without tests depending on your country ([§ 29 FeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__29.html)). → [38. Driving licence](../04-everyday-life/38-driving-licence.md)

**Can my spouse come?** Yes, subject to requirements such as finances and insurance; the application goes through the German mission in your family's country ([Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-recognition/family-reunification/spouses-joining-non-eu-citizens)). → [11. Family](../01-arriving-in-hannover/11-family-reunification.md)

**Can I stay after graduation?** Yes — non-EU graduates can get a job-seeker permit for up to 18 months ([§ 20 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__20.html)). → [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)

**Can I travel to other European countries with my residence card?** Yes, up to 90 days in any 180 in Schengen countries, without the right to work ([Federal Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/visa-und-aufenthalt/schengen-207786)). → [39. Travelling in Europe](../04-everyday-life/39-travel-schengen.md)

**🌍 My country is under sanctions — can I still pay my fees and open accounts?** Usually yes, but transfers may be slow or blocked. → [62. Sanctions and payment restrictions](../08-by-nationality/62-sanctions-payment-restrictions.md)

**🌍 I am a refugee / from Ukraine — can I study?** Yes; there are special advice services and rules. → [63. Refugees and people with protection status](../08-by-nationality/63-refugees-protection-status.md)
**The room ad says "no Anmeldung possible" — should I take it?** Only for the first few weeks. The landlord must legally confirm your move-in within two weeks, and without registration your bank account and residence permit are delayed ([§ 19 BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)). → [4. Accommodation](../01-arriving-in-hannover/04-housing.md)

**How much cash can I bring to Germany?** There is no limit, but €10,000 or more must be declared in writing to customs when you enter from outside the EU ([Zoll](https://www.zoll.de/EN/Private-individuals/Travel/Entering-Germany/Restrictions/Cash/cash_node.html)). → [3. Arriving in Hannover](../01-arriving-in-hannover/03-arrival-transport.md)

**QIS showed an error when I registered for an exam.** E-mail your faculty's examinations contact immediately and include your phone number; late registration is not accepted. → [18. Exams](../02-starting-your-studies/18-exams.md)

**Where can I see all important deadlines of the year?** → [65. Annual student calendar](65-annual-calendar.md)

**The bank won't open an account for me.** Try another branch or bank. Everyone legally resident in the EU has the right to a basic account, and the bank must respond within ten business days ([§ 31 ZKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zkg/__31.html)). → [6. Bank account](../01-arriving-in-hannover/06-bank-account.md)

**I signed a contract online or at my door and regret it.** You usually have a 14-day right of withdrawal and don't need to give a reason ([§ 355 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__355.html)). → [67. Consumer rights and free legal help](67-consumer-rights-legal-help.md)

**Where can I get free legal advice?** The Law Faculty's Legal Clinic advises LUH students free of charge ([Legal Clinic](https://www.jura.uni-hannover.de/de/legalclinic)). → [67](67-consumer-rights-legal-help.md)

**Where can I get certified copies of my university documents?** At the university's Beglaubigungsstelle — free, by appointment ([LUH – Certification office](https://www.uni-hannover.de/universitaet/campus-stadt/beglaubigungsstelle)). → [66. Documents, translations and certified copies](66-documents-translations-certified-copies.md)

**My passport is about to expire.** Renew it at your country's embassy or consulate before applying to extend your residence permit. → [66](66-documents-translations-certified-copies.md)

**I want to invite my parents to visit.** The host signs a Verpflichtungserklärung at the Ausländerbehörde; fee €29 ([City of Hannover](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/verpflichtungserklaerung-abgeben-1322-0.html?myMedium=1)). → [11. Family](../01-arriving-in-hannover/11-family-reunification.md)

**I'm turning 30 — what happens to my student health insurance?** Compulsory student insurance ends at the latest at 30 ([§ 5 SGB V](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_5/__5.html)). Ask your insurer early. → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)

**I saw the doctor late — can the sick note be backdated?** Only exceptionally, usually by up to three days. → [33. Doctors](../04-everyday-life/33-doctor-pharmacy.md)

**How do I find a doctor who speaks English?** Use the language filter of the official 116117 doctor search. → [33. Doctors](../04-everyday-life/33-doctor-pharmacy.md)
