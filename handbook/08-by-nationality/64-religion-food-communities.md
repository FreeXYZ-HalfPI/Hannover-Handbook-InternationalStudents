# 64. Religion, Food, Culture and Your Home Community

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 8 — Information by Nationality](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

Feeling at home matters. This page helps you find your embassy or consulate, places of worship, food from home and cultural communities in Hannover — and explains how religion is handled at German universities.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** international students — whatever their religion, culture or language.

## 3. Steps

**Your embassy or consulate**

1.  Find your country's embassy (usually in Berlin) and any consulate responsible for Lower Saxony in the Federal Foreign Office's list of foreign missions in Germany ([Federal Foreign Office – Missions of other states in Germany](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/reiseundsicherheit/vertretungen-anderer-staaten)). Several countries have honorary consulates in Hannover; for full consular services (passports) you may need to travel to Berlin, Hamburg or Frankfurt.
2.  Renew your passport **well before** it expires — your residence permit can only be extended with a valid passport. → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)
3.  🌍 **EU citizens** whose country has no embassy nearby can get consular help from any other EU member state's embassy ([Your Europe – Consular protection](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/consular-protection/index_en.htm)).

**Religion**

1.  **Freedom of religion** is guaranteed by the Basic Law, including the freedom to have no religion ([Basic Law – Article 4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_gg/englisch_gg.html#p0026)).
2.  **At university:** LUH is a secular institution. Exams can be scheduled on religious holidays; if this is a problem, ask your examinations office early. ⚠️ There is no general right to reschedule.
3.  **Church tax:** only deducted if you declared membership of a tax-collecting religious community at registration. → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)
4.  **University chaplaincies and religious student groups:** Protestant and Catholic university communities (ESG, KHG) are open to all and also offer social and financial advice; Muslim, Jewish, Hindu, Buddhist and other student groups are listed among the registered student associations ([LUH – Student associations](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/studentische-vereinigungen)). → [49. Student groups](../06-student-community/49-international-groups.md) ℹ️ Know of a room of silence or prayer room on campus? Please add it via [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md).
5.  **Places of worship in Hannover:** the city's information on religious communities and intercultural life is a starting point ([hannover.de – Living in Hannover](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover)). ℹ️ Community members: please add verified addresses on [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md).

**Food**

1.  **Canteen labels:** vegetarian/vegan dishes and allergens are marked; ask staff about pork, alcohol or halal options. → [28. Mensa](../03-university-services/28-mensa.md)
2.  **International groceries:** many shops sell food from around the world. → [31. Shopping](../04-everyday-life/31-shopping.md)

**Culture and community**

1.  **Intercultural life in Hannover:** many migrant organisations and cultural associations are active in the city; the city's integration and migration pages are a starting point ([hannover.de – Living in the Hannover region](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover)). ⚠️
2.  **Country-based student groups at LUH** → [49. Student groups](../06-student-community/49-international-groups.md)
3.  **Discrimination or racism:** you don't have to accept it. → [27. ChancenVielfalt](../03-university-services/27-chancenvielfalt.md)
