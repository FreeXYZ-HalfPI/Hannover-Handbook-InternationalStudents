## 1. Purpose

The **ZQS** (Zentrale Einrichtung für Qualitätsentwicklung in Studium und Lehre) helps students with **study methods, time and exam management, key skills and career planning** ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students — especially those adjusting to the German way of studying and examining, or looking for an internship.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Learning support (Lernwerkstatt):** advice and workshops on learning, time management and exams ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)).
2.  **Key competencies (Schlüsselkompetenzen):** seminars that earn **ECTS credits** and projects with companies or on sustainability ([ZQS – Key competencies](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc)). ℹ️ Intercultural-competence and academic-writing courses are especially useful for international students.
3.  **Career Service:** career guidance, internships, starting your career and the "Career Dates" job fair ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)). → [45. Career service](../05-work-finance-career/45-career-service.md)
4.  **Office hours at the Infothek:** learning advice Thursdays 13:00–14:00; Career Service Mondays 13:00–14:00 ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
