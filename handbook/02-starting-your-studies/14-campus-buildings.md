## 1. Purpose

LUH is spread across several parts of the city. This page helps you find the main building, lecture halls, library and canteens, and understand building numbers (e.g. 1101).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** new students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Main campus areas:** Welfengarten (main building), Conti-Campus at Königsworther Platz, Schneiderberg, Herrenhausen (the "green campus" for life, plant and earth sciences at Herrenhäuser Straße 2) and **Campus Maschinenbau Garbsen** for mechanical engineering ([LUH – Campus & city](https://www.uni-hannover.de/universitaet/campus-stadt/); [Faculty of Natural Sciences – Campus areas](https://www.naturwissenschaften.uni-hannover.de/fakultaet/campusbereiche)).
2.  **Getting to Garbsen:** Stadtbahn line 4 to "Schönebecker Allee", then bus 404 (weekdays) or about 500 m on foot ([Faculty of Natural Sciences – Campus areas](https://www.naturwissenschaften.uni-hannover.de/fakultaet/campusbereiche)).
3.  **Main building (Welfenschloss):** Welfengarten 1, 30167 Hannover; the ServiceCenter is on floor 01 next to the covered courtyard (Lichthof) ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)). → [21. ServiceCenter](../03-university-services/21-servicecenter.md)
4.  **Building and room numbers:** every building has a four-digit number (e.g. main building 1101, International Office 1106). Building details: [info.cafm.uni-hannover.de](https://info.cafm.uni-hannover.de/building/1101) ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)). ℹ️ Room "F 102" in building 1101 is often written "1101 – F102".
5.  **Map:** use the [Standortfinder](https://standortfinder.uni-hannover.de/map.php) ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
6.  **Explore the campus:** join the "Campus-Rallye" during orientation week (O-Woche) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
7.  **Library:** the university library is the **TIB**. Your LeibnizCard is your library card, but it must be **activated** at the library information desk with your passport and registration certificate ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). Online pre-registration: [TIB Reader Registration](https://reg.lbs.tib.eu/registration/tib/edit.php?lang=en).
8.  **Accessibility:** for step-free access and building accessibility, ask the advisory service for students with disabilities. → [27. ChancenVielfalt](../03-university-services/27-chancenvielfalt.md)
9.  Canteens on each campus → [28. Mensa](../03-university-services/28-mensa.md)
