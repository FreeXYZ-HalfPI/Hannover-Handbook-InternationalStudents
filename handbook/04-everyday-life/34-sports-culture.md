# 34. Leisure, Sports, Culture and Social Life

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-26 · **Section:** [Section 4 — Everyday Life in Hannover](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

Ways to do sport, enjoy culture and make friends in Hannover — from university sports to theatre and opera with a student ticket.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **University sports (Hochschulsport):** about 100 sports in hundreds of courses; registration opens shortly before the semester and popular courses fill up quickly ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist); [Hochschulsport – Sports programmes](https://www.hochschulsport-hannover.de/en/sports-programs/)).
2.  Students of LUH and partner universities pay **student rates** ([Hochschulsport](https://www.hochschulsport-hannover.de/en/university-sports-department/about-us)). Your LeibnizCard is your sports card ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
3.  **Theatre and opera:** LUH students can attend the Staatstheater Hannover with the "Theaterflatrate"; its share (Kulturticket, €2) is included in the semester fee ([Studentenwerk – Save money](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/money/save-money); [LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)). Details at [staatstheater-hannover.de](https://www.staatstheater-hannover.de/).
4.  **Student discounts** for cinemas and museums with your enrolment certificate ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [31. Shopping](31-shopping.md)
5.  **City and university:** [Campus and city](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt) and city events in the [hannover.de events calendar](https://www.hannover.de/Veranstaltungskalender). ℹ️ Popular free spots: Herrenhausen Gardens (reduced student entry), Maschsee lake, Eilenriede city forest, and the Maschsee festival and Schützenfest in summer.
6.  **Making friends:** Study-Buddy, international student groups and events → [48. Programmes for international students](../06-student-community/48-international-programmes-luh.md), [49. International student groups](../06-student-community/49-international-groups.md), [51. Events and networking](../06-student-community/51-events-networking.md)
7.  **Getting Theaterflatrate tickets:** free flatrate tickets are released **three days before** each performance in the theatre's online shop while seats last; people under 30 may also get up to 30% off, depending on the performance ([Staatstheater – Ticket service](https://staatstheater-hannover.de/de_DE/kartenservice)).
