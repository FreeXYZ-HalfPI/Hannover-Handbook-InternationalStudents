# 33. Doctors, Pharmacies and Health Care

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 4 — Everyday Life in Hannover](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

In Germany you usually see a **general practitioner (Hausarzt)** first, who refers you to a specialist if needed. This page explains how to find a doctor, where to go at night and at weekends, and how pharmacies work.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students with health insurance. → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)

## 3. Steps

1.  **Always carry the insurance card** your insurer sends you and show it at the practice. ℹ️ If you don't have the card yet, ask your insurer for a temporary certificate. 🌍 EU students with an EHIC show the EHIC.
2.  **Finding a doctor and booking appointments:** the official service **116117** (phone and [116117.de](https://www.116117.de/)) helps find doctors and arrange appointments. ℹ️ Many practices list the languages they speak; online booking platforms let you filter by language.
3.  **Nights, weekends and holidays (non-urgent):** the on-call medical service (ärztlicher Bereitschaftsdienst) via **116117** ([116117.de](https://www.116117.de/)).
4.  **Emergency and danger to life:** **112** → [37. Emergency numbers](37-emergency.md)
5.  **Pharmacy (Apotheke):** prescription medicines require a doctor's prescription (Rezept). Find the on-duty pharmacy with the free postcode search at **aponet.de**; pharmacies may charge €2.50 at night (usually 20:00–06:00), on Sundays and holidays, which your insurer pays if the doctor writes "noctu" on the prescription ([aponet – On-duty pharmacy search](https://www.aponet.de/notdienstsuche)).
6.  **Co-payment for medicines (Zuzahlung):** 10% of the price, at least €5 and at most €10, never more than the medicine itself; under-18s are exempt ([Federal Ministry of Health – Co-payment rules (PDF)](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/fileadmin/Dateien/3_Downloads/A/Arzneimittelversorgung/Zuzahlungsregelungen_GKV.pdf)).
7.  🌍 **Bringing medicines from home:** some medicines that are legal in your country are controlled in Germany. Carry a prescription or doctor's letter (in English or German); for narcotics you need a certificate under the Schengen rules or a doctor's certificate ([BfArM – Travelling with narcotic drugs](https://www.bfarm.de/EN/Federal-Opium-Agency/Narcotic-drugs/Travelling-with-narcotic-drugs/_node.html)).
8.  **Dentist:** check-ups are covered by statutory insurance; many treatments require a co-payment. ℹ️ Ask for a cost estimate (Heil- und Kostenplan) first.
9.  **Mental health:** free ptb counselling at the university → [29. Counselling](../03-university-services/29-counselling.md)

## 5. Responsible office

- **Official name:** Kassenärztliche Vereinigung — service **116117** (finding a doctor and on-call service)
- **Contact:** 116117 · [116117.de](https://www.116117.de/)
- **Your health insurer:** for questions on costs and coverage
