# 32. Internet, Mobile and Service Contracts

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 4 — Everyday Life in Hannover](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

After the first months many students sign contracts for home internet, mobile phone or electricity. German contracts have fixed terms and cancellation rules; this page summarises what to check before signing. (SIM card for the first days → [8. SIM card and internet](../01-arriving-in-hannover/08-sim-internet.md))

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students living in a private flat or shared flat (WG); many Studentenwerk halls include internet ([LUH – Housing](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Contract term and cancellation:** phone and internet contracts can have an initial term of at most **24 months**; after that they can be cancelled **monthly** with one month's notice. The last possible cancellation date must be shown on your bill ([Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/digitale-welt/mobilfunk-und-festnetz/wichtige-kundenrechte-fuer-telefon-handy-und-internetvertraege-65879); [Bundesnetzagentur – Contracts](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/TK/InternetTelefon/Vertrag/start.html)). ℹ️ If you will only stay 1–2 years, prefer monthly-cancellable plans.
2.  **Right of withdrawal:** contracts concluded online or by phone can usually be withdrawn within **14 days** without giving reasons ([§ 355 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__355.html)).
3.  **Student discounts:** some mobile providers offer discounts with an enrolment certificate ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
4.  **Electricity:** if you rent a private flat you need an electricity contract. ℹ️ In halls and many WGs electricity is included in the rent; ask your landlord.
5.  **Beware of door-to-door and phone sales:** don't sign anything at the door; ask for the offer in writing. ℹ️
6.  **Before leaving Germany or moving,** cancel contracts in time. ℹ️ → [47. Leaving Germany](../05-work-finance-career/47-leaving-germany.md)
7.  **Problem with a contract?** The consumer advice centre (Verbraucherzentrale Niedersachsen) helps; telephone advice without appointment is free, in-person advice needs an appointment ([Verbraucherzentrale – Hannover office](https://www.verbraucherzentrale-niedersachsen.de/beratungsstellen/beratungsstelle-hannover-121455?page=1)).
