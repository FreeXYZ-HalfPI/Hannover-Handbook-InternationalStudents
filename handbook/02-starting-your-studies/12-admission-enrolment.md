## 1. Purpose

Admission (Zulassung) does not yet make you a student; you only become a member of the university after **enrolment (Immatrikulation / Einschreibung)** ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)). This page explains the steps and documents.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** newly admitted students; 🌍 non-EU students submit more documents.
- **Before applying:** non-EU applicants with a foreign school certificate need a VPD from **uni-assist**. Bachelor's applicants with foreign certificates need German at **B1** to apply and **C1** to enrol, unless the programme regulations say otherwise ([LUH – Language requirements](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/sprachnachweise)). → [1. Applying and uni-assist](../01-arriving-in-hannover/01-application-uni-assist.md)

## 3. Steps

1.  **Know your programme type:** in programmes with **restricted admission (zulassungsbeschränkt)** you receive an admission letter (Zulassungsbescheid) and must accept it by the stated deadline. In programmes with **open admission (zulassungsfrei)** there is no admission letter and you enrol directly ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)).
2.  **Deadlines** are in your admission letter. Admission letters for the winter semester are sent from August, for the summer semester from February ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)).
3.  In the **applicant portal**, open the "Einschreibung" tab; forms and the upload link are there ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)).
4.  🌍 **Non-EU students:** scan all documents, combine them into **one PDF** named "SURNAME Firstname(s)\_Applicantnumber.pdf" and upload it via the portal ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)).
5.  **Pay the semester fee;** you are only enrolled once the **full amount** has arrived. For transfers from abroad, allow for bank charges ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)). → [13. Semester fee and LeibnizCard](13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
6.  Ask your health insurer to send the **electronic insurance notification** to the university, and upload a **photo** for your student card at [photo.idm.uni-hannover.de](https://photo.idm.uni-hannover.de/students/) ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)). → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)
7.  **Conditional admission:** if you have provisional admission (vorläufige Zulassung), you must meet its conditions (e.g. language certificate) by the deadline in your letter, otherwise your enrolment is cancelled ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
8.  **Visa delays:** if your visa will not arrive in time, contact the admissions office and the International Office **before** the enrolment deadline — some programmes allow a later start or deferral. ⚠️ Rules vary by programme; ask in writing.
9.  **After enrolment:** in September (winter) or March (summer) your enrolment documents and LeibnizCard arrive by post, together with an invitation to the welcome event ([LUH – Enrolment](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/nach-der-bewerbung/einschreibung)).
