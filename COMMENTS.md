# Comment rules

Under *7. Student experiences (unofficial)* on every page of the website you can write your own experience or comment. This page says what is welcome, what is removed and where the data goes.

## How it works

- Comments are stored with [utterances](https://utteranc.es) as **comments under an issue** in this repository (label `💬 comments`). They are therefore **public** and appear under **your GitHub username**.
- The comment box loads only after you click "Show comments". Until then your browser contacts neither `utteranc.es` nor GitHub.
- Comments are **personal experience**, not law, not legal advice and not an official source. If a comment turns out to be right, the team checks it against the official source and puts it on the page.

## What to post

- What you did, **in which year**, with which office, and what happened.
- A correction to a figure or deadline, ideally with a link to the official source.
- Waiting times and tips that help the next student.

## What not to post

- Personal data of yourself or others: addresses, case numbers, passport numbers, dates of birth, phone numbers, tracking codes.
- Names of staff or other individuals in a negative story; insults, accusations, advertising and links to paid services.
- Ways to get around the rules, forged documents or anything that could put you or others at risk.
- Anything about **other people's residence or military-service status**.

## Sensitive topics (consulate, military service, residence status)

Your comment is public and may stay in search engines and archives. If you write about an embassy, military service or your residence status, leave out details that identify you, and consider using an account whose name is not your real name. If you do not want something to be public, do not write it.

## Moderation and removal

- The maintainers may **edit, hide or delete** comments that break these rules and lock issues.
- You can edit or delete your own comment on GitHub at any time. To ask us to remove one, [open an issue](https://github.com/FreeXYZ-HalfPI/Hannover-Handbook-InternationalStudents/issues/new/choose) or write in the [ISL Telegram channel](https://t.me/hannover_iranian_students); we follow up.
- You can also use "Report" on the comment on GitHub.

## Data

Comments are stored by GitHub ([GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)). After you click "Show comments" your browser opens `utteranc.es` and GitHub. This project keeps no other visitor data.
