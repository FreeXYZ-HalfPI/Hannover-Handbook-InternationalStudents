# 48. Programmes for International Students at LUH — Study-Buddy, Senior Student Partnership and More

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 6 — Student Community & Engagement](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

LUH runs several support and mentoring programmes specifically for international students. This page collects them in one place so you can sign up early — ideally before you arrive.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** international degree students (EU and non-EU), especially in the first two semesters.
- German and senior international students who want to volunteer as buddies or mentors.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Study-Buddy:** the International Office pairs new international students with students already at LUH, who help with first steps and show you around; local and international students sign up separately ([LUH – Study-Buddy](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende/betreuungsangebote/study-buddy)). ℹ️ Register as soon as you have your admission letter.
2.  **Senior Student Partnership:** peer mentoring in which experienced students support international newcomers during their studies ([LUH – Senior Student Partnership](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende/betreuungsangebote/senior-student-partnership)).
3.  **Care services overview:** all support offers for international students ([LUH – Care services](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende/betreuungsangebote)).
4.  **Orientation Days, Start Smart and STEPin** → [51. Events and networking](51-events-networking.md)
5.  **Events and excursions** for international students (city tours, trips, cultural evenings) ([LUH – Events and excursions](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/internationales-an-der-luh/veranstaltungen)).
6.  **International Day:** an annual event presenting international opportunities at LUH ([LUH – International Day](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/aktuelle-termine/internationaler-tag)).
7.  **EasyStartLUH:** a personal digital roadmap for your study start ([EasyStartLUH](https://go.lu-h.de/EasyStartLUH)).
8.  **Information for international graduates:** what to do at the end of your studies ([LUH – After your studies](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/nach-dem-studium/after-your-studies)). → [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)
