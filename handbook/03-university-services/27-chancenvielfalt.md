# 27. Office for Equal Opportunities and Diversity — Hochschulbüro für ChancenVielfalt

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 3 — University Services](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

The **Hochschulbüro für ChancenVielfalt** is the university's central office for equal opportunities and diversity. It advises all students on topics from **family and studying with children** to **experiences of discrimination** — including racism and discrimination based on origin, religion, gender or sexual orientation ([ChancenVielfalt – Counselling](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/de/beratung)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students — especially those who have experienced discrimination or harassment, student parents, LGBTQIA+ students and students with a disability or chronic illness.

## 3. Steps

1.  Book an appointment by e-mail or phone; advice is available **in person or online** and also **in English** ([ChancenVielfalt](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/de/beratung)).
2.  **Confidentiality:** nothing is passed on without your consent; **anonymous** reporting via a form is also possible ([ChancenVielfalt](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/de/beratung)).
3.  **Protection against discrimination:** learn about your rights and options ([Protection against discrimination](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/beratung/diskriminierungsschutz)).
4.  **A-Team:** a confidential student-to-student contact point for experiences of discrimination ([ChancenVielfalt](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/de/beratung)).
5.  **Studying with children:** [Studying with a child](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/familienservice/studieren-mit-kind).
6.  **Students with disabilities or chronic illness:** the university has a Commissioner for Students with Disabilities, and ChancenVielfalt can refer you ([Referral advice](https://www.chancenvielfalt.uni-hannover.de/beratung/verweisberatung)). More: [Inclusive studies](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/beratung-hilfe/inklusiv-studieren). 🌍 International applicants with a disability can also contact the national information centre at the Deutsches Studierendenwerk ([DSW – Studying with disabilities](https://www.studierendenwerke.de/en/topics/studying-with-disabilities)).
7.  **Outside the university:** the Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency offers free advice in several languages ([Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency](https://www.antidiskriminierungsstelle.de/EN/homepage/homepage-node.html)).
