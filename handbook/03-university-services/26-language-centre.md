# 26. University Language Centre — Leibniz Language Centre (LLC)

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 3 — University Services](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

The **Leibniz Language Centre (LLC)** offers general and specialised language courses, exam preparation, placement tests and writing support in foreign languages ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students learning **German** or preparing for the DSH exam. → [35. Learning German](../04-everyday-life/35-learning-german.md)
- Any student who wants English, French, Spanish or other languages.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Languages:** English, French, Spanish, German and others such as Italian and Turkish ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
2.  **German:** semester courses ([LLC – German courses](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-courses/german)) and the intensive course "D-intensiv" preparing for the **DSH** ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
3.  **Registration only via Stud.IP.** For German, English and Spanish (except A1) you must pass a **placement test** in Stud.IP; TOEFL or DSH certificates are not accepted instead ([LLC – Registration](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-courses/language-course-registration)).
4.  **Registration period:** for winter 2026/27 it ran from 25 September to 16 October 2026, followed by a lottery ([LLC – Registration](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-courses/language-course-registration)). ℹ️ Check the same page for the next semester.
5.  If your placement result does not match your actual level: placement@llc.uni-hannover.de ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
6.  **Exams:** DSH, TOEFL and the DAAD language certificate ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
7.  **Self-directed learning (SDL)** and **tandem** partnerships — a great way to practise German while teaching your own language ([Tandem offers](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-courses/tandem-learning/tandem-learning-offers)).
8.  **InterWRITE:** academic writing support in a foreign language ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
