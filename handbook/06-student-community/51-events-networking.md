## 1. Purpose

Finding friends and a professional network in your first months — loneliness is one of the most common problems for new students ([ptb](https://www.ptb.uni-hannover.de/en/)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students, especially in the first semester.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Erstival:** the central welcome event where the university president greets new students; the invitation comes with your enrolment documents ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist); [Erstival](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/erstival)).
2.  **Orientation week (O-Woche)** and **Orientation Days** for international students ([Orientation Days](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/preparation-courses-and-introductory-sessions/orientation-days)).
3.  **Networking from day one:** the university's page [Making contacts](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/kontakte-knuepfen) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
4.  **Start Smart:** online events for first-year Bachelor's and Master's students; some need registration ([Start Smart](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/start-smart)).
5.  **AStA intercultural events** ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)) and **Career Dates** for professional networking ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)). → [45. Career service](../05-work-finance-career/45-career-service.md)
6.  **International Office events and excursions** → [48. Programmes for international students](48-international-programmes-luh.md)
7.  **University social media:** [Instagram @uni_hannover](https://www.instagram.com/uni_hannover/) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/school/leibniz-universit-t-hannover-germany) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
8.  **Country and culture groups** → [49. International student groups](49-international-groups.md)
