## 1. Purpose

Finishing your studies or leaving Germany has its own paperwork. If it is not done, bills and contracts may continue after you leave. This page lists what to do.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students who have finished or are leaving Germany — and those just moving to another city (new registration → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Deregister your address (Abmeldung):** if you are not moving to a new address in Germany, deregister within **two weeks** of moving out; at the earliest one week before ([§ 17 BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__17.html)). In the city of Hannover this is **free** ([hannover.gov.de – Deregistration](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/abmeldung-einer-wohnung-wegzug-aus-hannover-900000020-0.html?myMedium=1)). Keep the deregistration certificate — banks, insurers and the broadcasting fee service ask for it.
2.  **Broadcasting fee:** deregister ([Rundfunkbeitrag – Deregister](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/buergerinnen-und-buerger/formulare/abmelden)). → [9. Tax ID and official letters](../01-arriving-in-hannover/09-tax-id-broadcasting-fee.md)
3.  **Contracts:** cancel phone, internet, electricity and gym in time; phone and internet contracts can be cancelled with one month's notice after the minimum term ([Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/digitale-welt/mobilfunk-und-festnetz/wichtige-kundenrechte-fuer-telefon-handy-und-internetvertraege-65879)). → [32. Contracts](../04-everyday-life/32-contracts.md)
4.  **Housing:** give notice according to your contract and get your deposit back. ℹ️ The notice period is in your contract (usually three months for private flats). → [4. Accommodation](../01-arriving-in-hannover/04-housing.md)
5.  **Health insurance:** tell your insurer that you are finishing or leaving. ℹ️ Student insurance ends after de-registration from the university; ask your insurer for details. → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)
6.  **University:** de-registration (Exmatrikulation) and returning library items. → [19. Leave and withdrawal](../02-starting-your-studies/19-leave-change-withdrawal.md)
7.  **Bank account and tax:** close the account after the last bills are paid and check a tax return for your final year to reclaim overpaid tax. → [6. Bank account](../01-arriving-in-hannover/06-bank-account.md), [42. Tax](42-tax-payslip.md)
8.  **Blocked account:** close it with your provider once you no longer need it; any remaining balance is paid out. ℹ️
9.  🌍 **Pension contributions refund:** non-EU citizens who leave Germany permanently can usually apply for a refund of their own pension contributions after **24 months** outside Germany — **not** if you are from a country with a social security agreement that counts German periods (e.g. USA, Canada, India, Japan, Korea, Türkiye) or an EU/EEA country ([Deutsche Rentenversicherung – Refund of contributions (German)](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Rente/Allgemeine-Informationen/Beitragserstattung/beitragserstattung_node.html)). ⚠️ Check your case with the DRV.
10. **If you want to stay in Germany** → [46. After graduation](46-after-graduation.md)

## 5. Responsible office

- **Deregistration:** citizens' offices (Bürgerämter) of the city of Hannover → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md) ([hannover.gov.de](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/abmeldung-einer-wohnung-wegzug-aus-hannover-900000020-0.html?myMedium=1))
- **Broadcasting fee:** ARD ZDF Deutschlandradio Beitragsservice — [rundfunkbeitrag.de](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/)
- **Pension refund:** Deutsche Rentenversicherung — [deutsche-rentenversicherung.de](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/)
