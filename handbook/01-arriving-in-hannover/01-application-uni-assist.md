# 1. Applying, uni-assist and Recognition of Your Certificates

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 1 — Arriving in Hannover & First Steps](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

The first step is getting admitted. This page explains how to apply to Leibniz University Hannover (LUH), when the deadlines are, what uni-assist and the VPD are, and how foreign school and university certificates are assessed.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Bachelor's and Master's applicants who are still abroad or already in Germany.
- Graduates who need their foreign degree assessed for work or further study.
- 🌍 Rules differ for EU/EEA applicants, applicants with a German school-leaving certificate and applicants from APS countries (China, India, Vietnam) — see [Section 8 — Information by nationality](../08-by-nationality/README.md).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Find your programme and its requirements.** Entry and language requirements are set in each programme's admission regulations, and only those regulations are binding ([LUH – Language requirements](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/sprachnachweise)). Programme search: [LUH – Degree programmes](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/studienangebot).
2.  **Deadlines.** Many Master's programmes have earlier deadlines for **non-EU applicants**: **15 April – 31 May** for the winter semester and **15 October – 30 November** of the previous year for the summer semester (not every programme starts in summer). German and EU applicants usually have until 15 July / 15 January. Some programmes are exceptions ([LUH – Overview of application deadlines (PDF)](https://www.uni-hannover.de/fileadmin/Studienberatung/Neu_ab_Relaunch/02_Studium_Vor_dem_Studium/02_Bewerbung_und_Zulassung/02_Studienplatzbewerbung/03_MA-Bewerbung_D_EU/Master_Bewerbungsfriste-alleSem-engl.pdf); [LUH – Application & admission](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung)). ⚠️ Always check the current year's deadline for your own programme.
3.  **VPD from uni-assist.** Non-EU applicants with foreign certificates usually need a **preliminary review documentation (VPD)** from uni-assist, which they must submit to the university themselves before the deadline ([uni-assist – VPD](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/vpd/); [LUH – Language requirements](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/sprachnachweise)).
4.  **VPD timing.** A VPD usually takes 4–6 weeks after your documents and payment arrive; send everything at least **8 weeks before the deadline**. A VPD is valid for **one year** from issue ([uni-assist – FAQs](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/faqs/plan-your-application/)). 🌍 uni-assist publishes country-specific document requirements: [uni-assist – Country information](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/info-country-by-country/).
5.  **Translations.** Documents not in German or English need a certified translation into German or English, together with the original-language document ([uni-assist – FAQs](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/faqs/plan-your-application/)).
6.  **Language.** Bachelor's applicants with foreign certificates need German at **B1** to apply and **C1** to enrol, unless the programme regulations say otherwise. English-taught Master's programmes set their own English requirements ([LUH – Language requirements](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/sprachnachweise)). → [35. Learning German](../04-everyday-life/35-learning-german.md)
7.  **Does your school certificate give direct access?** Check the free **anabin** database (school certificates and universities by country). If your certificate does not give direct university access, you may need the **Feststellungsprüfung** after a year at a preparatory college (**Studienkolleg**) — Lower Saxony's is in Hannover ([anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/); [Niedersächsisches Studienkolleg](https://www.stk.uni-hannover.de/)).
8.  **Assessment of a foreign university degree.** Check anabin first; if your university or degree is not listed, the **ZAB** issues a "Statement of Comparability" (Zeugnisbewertung). This is a comparison, not a professional licence ([Recognition in Germany – Assessment of higher education qualifications](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/pro/assessment-higher-education-qualifications.php); [ZAB – Fees](https://zab.kmk.org/en/statement-comparability/fees)).
9.  **After admission:** visa, proof of finances and insurance → [2. Before you arrive](02-before-arrival-visa.md); enrolment → [12. Admission and enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md).

## 5. Responsible office

- **Preliminary review:** uni-assist e.V. — [uni-assist.de](https://www.uni-assist.de/)
- **Admission and advice:** Leibniz University Hannover — Central Student Advisory Service → [21. ServiceCenter](../03-university-services/21-servicecenter.md); International Office → [22. International Office](../03-university-services/22-international-office.md)
- **Degree assessment:** Zentralstelle für ausländisches Bildungswesen (ZAB) — [zab.kmk.org](https://zab.kmk.org/)
