## 1. Purpose

In Germany students have a **share and representatives** in running the university. This page explains the structure of student representation at LUH and how to take part.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students who want student voices — including international students' voices — to be heard.
- **Voting rights:** all enrolled students, **regardless of nationality**, can vote in student elections ([LUH – Bodies & committees](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/organe-gremien)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Student body (Studierendenschaft):** all students are members, and part of the semester fee (€15.80) goes to it ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).
2.  **Student Council (Studentischer Rat, StuRa):** a kind of student parliament made up of representatives of the faculties' Fachschaft councils and members elected directly by all students. Elections are online, and terms last two semesters ([LUH – Bodies & committees](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/organe-gremien); [Student body election rules (PDF)](https://www.uni-hannover.de/fileadmin/luh/content/wahlamt/dokumente/Wahl-_und_andere_Ordnungen/VKBl_Nr._08_2025_-_Wahlordnung_der_Studierendenschaft.pdf)).
3.  **AStA:** the executive committee of the student body; it is not elected directly by students but by the StuRa, to which it is accountable ([LUH – Bodies & committees](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/organe-gremien)). → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)
4.  **Fachschaft and Fachrat:** student representation for each subject; Fachschaft councils are elected in the student elections ([Fachschaften](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/organe-gremien/fachschaften)). → [20. Fachschaft](../02-starting-your-studies/20-fachschaft.md)
5.  **Foreign students' commission (AusländerInnenkommission):** supported by AStA; advises university committees on improving the situation of international students ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)).
6.  **University bodies (Senate and faculty councils):** include student representatives. ⚠️ Check the number of seats and election dates at [Bodies & committees](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/organe-gremien).
7.  **Statutes and regulations:** [AStA – Statutes and regulations](https://www.asta-hannover.de/hochschulpolitik/satzungen-und-ordnungen/).
8.  🌍 **Political activity:** non-EU citizens enjoy freedom of expression and assembly in Germany; political activity at university is legal. ℹ️ If you fear consequences from your home country's authorities, talk confidentially to the International Office or AStA.
