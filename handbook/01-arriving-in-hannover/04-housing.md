# 4. Finding Accommodation and Signing a Rental Contract

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 1 — Arriving in Hannover & First Steps](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

Finding a room in Hannover is hard, especially at the start of the semester. This page summarises official and common ways to find a room, typical costs and what to check in a rental contract.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** new students.
- Studentenwerk halls of residence are only for students enrolled at a Hannover university ([LUH – Housing](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Studentenwerk halls:** 20 halls, mostly furnished, often with fast and cheap internet; rent including utilities is usually €240–500 per month ([LUH – Housing](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)). Waiting lists are very long at the start of the semester — apply early ([Studentenwerk – Housing search](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/wohnungssuche)).
2.  **Private student residences:** about €500–700 per month, e.g. Student One, THE FIZZ, i Live, Imvio and C5 ([LUH – Housing](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).
3.  **Room in a shared flat (WG):** common and a little more expensive than a hall; kitchen and bathroom are shared. Sources: Studentenwerk's online housing exchange and wg-gesucht.de ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/wohnungssuche); [LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).
4.  **Sublets (Zwischenmiete):** furnished rooms for a limited time — a good option for the first weeks ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).
5.  **Notice boards (Schwarzes Brett)** in the main building, canteens, libraries and institutes, and housing ads in Hannover newspapers on Wednesdays and Saturdays ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).
6.  **Outside Hannover:** Studentenwerk suggests also searching in towns on S-Bahn lines within about 50 km, e.g. Nienburg, Hameln, Peine, Celle and Hildesheim — the semester ticket covers the trains ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/wohnungssuche)).
7.  **The International Office accommodation service** is only for exchange students, doctoral candidates and researchers, and is not guaranteed ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).
8.  **Before signing,** make sure the landlord will give you a **landlord's confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung)**; without it you cannot register your address ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)). → [5. Registering your address](05-anmeldung.md)
9.  **Deposit (Kaution):** at most three months' rent excluding utilities; tenants may pay it in three monthly instalments, the first at the start of the tenancy ([§ 551 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__551.html)).
10. **Discrimination:** refusing a tenant because of ethnic origin is unlawful under the General Equal Treatment Act. If it happens to you, the Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency offers free advice ([Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency – Housing](https://www.antidiskriminierungsstelle.de/EN/about-discrimination/areas-of-life/daily-business/housing-market/housing-market-node.html)).
11. **Tenant advice:** AStA offers free legal advice for students; tenants' associations (Mieterverein) offer advice for members ([AStA Hannover](https://www.asta-hannover.de/)). → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)

> [!WARNING]
> **Rental scams:** never send money before you have seen the room and signed a proper contract. If someone asks for a deposit or first rent before a viewing, or the landlord is "abroad" and only reachable remotely, it is very likely a scam. If you have already paid, report it to the police immediately and ask your bank to stop the transfer ([Police information sheet via KIT (PDF, German)](https://www.intl.kit.edu/download/INFOBLATT_Betrug%20bei%20der%20Wohnungssuche.pdf)).
