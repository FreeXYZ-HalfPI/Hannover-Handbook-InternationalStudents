## 1. Purpose

An entry visa is limited in time. To stay for your studies you need a **residence permit for study purposes** (§ 16b AufenthG), issued as an **electronic residence permit card (eAT)** ([Studentenwerk Hannover – First steps](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- 🌍 **Non-EU students** staying longer than 90 days — both those who entered with a visa and those from visa-free countries (Australia, Canada, Israel, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, UK, USA and others) ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt); [§ 41 AufenthV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthv/__41.html)). → [60. Visa-free nationalities](../08-by-nationality/60-visa-free-nationalities.md)
- 🌍 **EU/EEA citizens** do not need one. **Swiss citizens** must apply for a (free-movement) residence permit for stays over 90 days ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). → [59. EU, EEA and Swiss citizens](../08-by-nationality/59-eu-eea-swiss.md)
- 🌍 Students with a study permit from **another EU country** may be able to study in Germany for up to 360 days under the EU mobility scheme without a German permit ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
- 🌍 Refugees and people with temporary protection (e.g. from Ukraine) keep their existing status → [63. Refugees and people with protection status](../08-by-nationality/63-refugees-protection-status.md).

## 3. Steps

1.  **When to apply:** within your first 90 days or **before your visa expires**, whichever is earlier. Apply early; delays are likely ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)).
2.  **Which office?** Residents of the **city of Hannover** go to the city's Ausländerbehörde (Landeshauptstadt Hannover); residents of the **Hannover region** (e.g. Garbsen) go to the Ausländerbehörde der Region Hannover ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt); [Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).
3.  **Online application (city of Hannover):** most applications are submitted through the city's online portal; no visit is needed at this stage ([hannover.de – Service for skilled workers, students and self-employed](https://www.hannover.de/Wirtschaft-Wissenschaft/Arbeit/Vereinbarkeit-von-Familie-und-Beruf/Unternehmen/Personal-und-Mitarbeitervermittlung/Kundenservice-f%C3%BCr-Fachkr%C3%A4fte,-Studierende-und-Selbstst%C3%A4ndige-sowie-deren-Familienangeh%C3%B6rige)). Portal: [Issue or extension of a residence permit](https://hannover.gov.de/portal/seiten/erteilung-oder-verlaengerung-einer-aufenthaltserlaubnis-oder-blauen-karte-eu-1141-30810.html?rubrik=1001).
    - **Finding the form:** in the portal enter your postcode, then choose "Aufenthaltserlaubnis für Studium, Ausbildung oder Schulbesuch" and "Aufenthaltserlaubnis zum Zwecke des Studiums oder für studienvorbereitende Maßnahmen". Choose **"Ersterteilung"** for your first permit and **"Verlängerung"** for an extension; for an extension also enter your **Aktenzeichen** (file number from your last letter) and upload the documents ([City of Hannover – Residence permit for study, online application](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/aufenthaltserlaubnis-zum-zwecke-des-studiums-und-fuer-studienvorbereitende-massnahmen-1347-0.html?myMedium=1)).
    - **No online access?** According to the office's checklist you can send the documents by e-mail to abh-fus@hannover-stadt.de (subject: "Antrag für Aufenthaltserlaubnis") or by post (checklist "Studium / Studienvorbereitung", City of Hannover, 2025 — PDF).
    - **Language:** documents and information must in principle be provided in German ([City of Hannover](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/aufenthaltserlaubnis-zum-zwecke-des-studiums-und-fuer-studienvorbereitende-massnahmen-1347-0.html?myMedium=1)).
    - **Processing time:** up to **3 months** once all documents have arrived ([City of Hannover](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/aufenthaltserlaubnis-zum-zwecke-des-studiums-und-fuer-studienvorbereitende-massnahmen-1347-0.html?myMedium=1)). The office recommends applying **at least 3 months before** your visa or permit expires (Willkommensservice presentation for students, 2025 — PDF).
    - **Length of the permit:** usually issued and extended for **two years**, at least one year; shorter only if your programme is shorter ([§ 16b AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html)).
    - **Your duty to cooperate:** you must provide the required documents without delay ([§ 82 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__82.html)); false or incomplete information can lead to revocation, a fine or expulsion ([City of Hannover](https://hannover.gov.de/buergerservice/dienstleistungen/aufenthaltserlaubnis-zum-zwecke-des-studiums-und-fuer-studienvorbereitende-massnahmen-1347-0.html?myMedium=1)).
4.  The office reviews the application and, if approved, contacts you for an **appointment** to give fingerprints and later collect the card; this step is in person ([hannover.de](https://www.hannover.de/Wirtschaft-Wissenschaft/Arbeit/Vereinbarkeit-von-Familie-und-Beruf/Unternehmen/Personal-und-Mitarbeitervermittlung/Kundenservice-f%C3%BCr-Fachkr%C3%A4fte,-Studierende-und-Selbstst%C3%A4ndige-sowie-deren-Familienangeh%C3%B6rige)).
5.  **If processing takes long:** if you apply for issue or extension **before your current permit or visa expires**, your stay remains lawful until the decision and you receive a **fictional certificate (Fiktionsbescheinigung)** ([§ 81 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__81.html)). This does not apply to Schengen visas. ℹ️ Keep proof that you applied (confirmation e-mail or PDF).
6.  **Extension from the 4th semester on:** current transcripts from the examinations office and a statement from your faculty about your study progress are required ([Studentenwerk](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany)).
7.  **Travel:** with a valid eAT you can travel in the Schengen area for up to 90 days in any 180. → [39. Travelling in Europe](../04-everyday-life/39-travel-schengen.md)
8.  **Working** on a student residence permit is limited. → [40. Student jobs](../05-work-finance-career/40-student-jobs.md)
9.  **After graduation** you can get a residence permit to **look for work for up to 18 months**. → [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)
10. **Free advice:** the university holds information events on immigration law for students ([LUH – Events](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/aktuelles/veranstaltungen)); the Refugee Law Clinic Hannover also advises migrants ([hannover.de – Refugee Law Clinic at the Faculty of Law (2020 report)](https://www.hannover.de/Service/Presse-Medien/Hannover.de/Aktuelles/Wirtschaft-Wissenschaft-2020/Migration-%E2%80%93-Perspektiven-der-Forschung)).

**Related pages:** [19. Leave, change of programme and withdrawal](../02-starting-your-studies/19-leave-change-withdrawal.md), [39. Travelling in Europe](../04-everyday-life/39-travel-schengen.md), [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)

## 7. Student experiences (unofficial)

> [!NOTE]
> This section is for personal experiences; it is not a general rule or legal advice. Share yours (with a date) on [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md) or by opening a GitHub issue.

- **Online extension (2025–2026):** the International Office prepared a step-by-step guide with screenshots for online extensions under §16b and §18b. Common mistakes: forgetting the Aktenzeichen, and not uploading the transcript (Leistungsnachweis) from the 4th semester on.
- **Online information session:** each semester, during orientation week, the Ausländerbehörde holds an information session for new international students (e.g. 26.03.2025). Attending answers many questions.
- **Slow replies to e-mails and online applications** — from a few days to several months. When their permit was about to expire, many students went **in person, early in the morning**, during opening hours and received a temporary certificate or Fiktionsbescheinigung the same day.
- **Fiktionsbescheinigung for urgent travel:** bring proof of the reason (e.g. ticket or employer's letter). Some reported that the Fiktion invoice already included an advance on the card fee.
- **Photos:** in 2025 and 2026 the invitation letter said to take the biometric photo in the building before the appointment, or to bring a digital photo from an approved photographer.
- **Collecting the card:** the new card was usually handed over in person and the old one taken back. If you hear nothing by the announced date, follow up in person.
- **Financial proof:** whether an employment contract was accepted instead of a blocked account — especially for a **first** application — varied from case to case. Rely only on the official checklist.
- **Passport validity:** if your passport expires before the end of the permit you are applying for, renew it first. → [66. Documents, translations and certified copies](../07-resources/66-documents-translations-certified-copies.md)
