## 1. Purpose

Unwanted contracts, auto-renewing subscriptions, instalment purchases, deposits that were never returned, threatening e-mails and "where do I find a free lawyer?" are among the most common problems students report. This page collects your basic rights and the places that help for free.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students — especially newcomers who don't read German yet and sign German contracts.

## 3. Steps

### A) Before you sign

1.  **Don't sign what you don't understand.** Take a copy home and translate it. Find the contract term, automatic renewal and the notice period (Kündigungsfrist). → [32. Contracts](../04-everyday-life/32-contracts.md)
2.  **Deposits and rent:** don't send money without a **written contract** and before you have seen the flat or received the keys; state in the transfer what the money is for. A tenant may only sublet to you **with the landlord's permission** ([§ 540 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__540.html)). → [4. Accommodation](../01-arriving-in-hannover/04-housing.md)
3.  **Instalment purchases:** compare the monthly instalment with your real income — if your student job ends, the instalments continue. ℹ️

### B) After you sign: the 14-day right of withdrawal

1.  Contracts made **online, by phone or away from business premises** (e.g. in a café or at your door) usually come with a **right of withdrawal (Widerrufsrecht)** ([§ 312g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312g.html)).
2.  The withdrawal period is **14 days** and usually starts when the contract is made. It is enough to **send** the withdrawal before the deadline, and you don't need to give a reason ([§ 355 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__355.html)). Withdraw in writing (e-mail or letter) and keep proof.
3.  **Exceptions:** custom-made goods, perishable goods, leisure or travel services for a fixed date and some other cases have no right of withdrawal ([§ 312g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312g.html)).

### C) Payment demands, debt collectors (Inkasso) and court payment orders (Mahnbescheid)

1.  **Threatening e-mail or letter from a person or a debt-collection agency (Inkasso):** neither ignoring it completely nor paying immediately is the right move. First check whether a contract actually exists, then ask the consumer advice centre. ℹ️
2.  **Take a Mahnbescheid seriously** — a court payment order in an official yellow envelope. You have **two weeks from delivery** to object (Widerspruch) ([§ 692 ZPO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__692.html)).

### D) The bank won't open an account

- Everyone legally resident in the EU has the right to a **basic payment account (Basiskonto)**; the bank must respond within ten business days ([§ 31 ZKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zkg/__31.html)). → [6. Bank account](../01-arriving-in-hannover/06-bank-account.md)

### E) Free help in Hannover

1.  **Consumer advice centre of Lower Saxony (Verbraucherzentrale):** contracts, subscriptions, energy, money matters; **free phone advice**, in-person advice by online appointment ([Verbraucherzentrale – Hannover advice centre](https://www.verbraucherzentrale-niedersachsen.de/beratungsstellen/beratungsstelle-hannover-121455?page=1)).
2.  **Legal Clinic of the LUH Faculty of Law:** **free, out-of-court legal advice** for students of all LUH faculties, given by law students supervised by experienced lawyers ([Legal Clinic Hannover](https://www.jura.uni-hannover.de/de/legalclinic)).
3.  **Studentenwerk Hannover:** **free legal advice** for international students ([hannover.de – Services for international students](https://www.hannover.de/Wirtschaft-Wissenschaft/Wissenschaft/Studieren-in-Hannover/Internationales/Service-f%C3%BCr-ausl%C3%A4ndische-Studierende)); social counselling by online appointment ([Studentenwerk – Advice](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/beratung/uebersicht)). → [23. Studentenwerk](../03-university-services/23-studentenwerk.md)
4.  **AStA:** advice for international students, including employment law. → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)

### F) Theft, threats and fraud

1.  **Immediate danger:** police **110**. → [37. Emergency numbers](../04-everyday-life/37-emergency.md)
2.  **Non-urgent cases** (theft in a hall of residence, online fraud): file a report (Anzeige) at the nearest police station or at the [Lower Saxony police online station](https://www.onlinewache.polizei.niedersachsen.de/); keep the evidence (messages, transfer receipts, the ad).
3.  **Theft or disputes in a hall of residence:** also tell the hall management or Studentenwerk. ℹ️

## 7. Student experiences (unofficial)

> [!NOTE]
> This section is for personal experiences; it is not a general rule or legal advice. Share yours (with a date) on [page 57](57-contribute-experiences.md) or by opening a GitHub issue.

- **Online fitness coach (2026):** a student signed a German contract in a café and later received threatening e-mails. Advice from other students: ask for a copy of the contract before any payment, don't reply emotionally to threats, and call the Verbraucherzentrale.
- **€100 "room reservation" deposit without a contract:** a common scam pattern.
- **Disputes with a main tenant (Hauptmieter):** several students got help from the university's Legal Clinic — you send your question by e-mail and get an appointment if they can take the case.
- **Ending a hall contract:** get the exact end date of the contract in writing before handing back the keys, to avoid paying rent for extra months.
