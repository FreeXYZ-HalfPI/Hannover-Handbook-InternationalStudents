## 1. Purpose

All the deadlines and events that come back every year, in one month-by-month table. Most reminders in student group chats are about exactly these dates.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** LUH students, especially in their first semesters.
- Student groups planning reminders and events.

## 3. Steps

1.  The "usually" column is based on the years 2024–2026. ℹ️ Always check the exact date for the current year in the official source of that row.
2.  Deadlines marked 🔴 are **not extended** and missing them has serious consequences. Set a reminder on your phone for them.

3.  🌍 **Cultural festivals of student groups** — for example Nowruz (around 20 March) and Yalda night (21 December) celebrated by the [Iranian Student Association (ISL)](https://t.me/hannover_iranian_students) with the International Office — are open to all students; most need registration. → [49. International student groups](../06-student-community/49-international-groups.md)

### Public holidays in Lower Saxony

4.  State-wide public holidays: **1 January, Good Friday (Karfreitag), Easter Monday (Ostermontag), 1 May, Ascension Day (Himmelfahrt), Whit Monday (Pfingstmontag), 3 October (German Unity Day), 31 October (Reformation Day), 25 and 26 December** ([NFeiertagsG § 2](https://umwelt-online.de/recht/arbeitss/arbeitsrecht/laender/nds/feiertg.htm); [Draft law on Reformation Day](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/128351/Entwurf_eines_Gesetzes_zur_Aenderung_des_Niedersaechsischen_Gesetzes_ueber_die_Feiertage.pdf)).
5.  Shops are closed on these days and on Sundays. When a holiday falls on a Saturday or Monday you may not be able to shop for two or three days in a row — shop in advance. → [31. Shopping](../04-everyday-life/31-shopping.md)
6.  Libraries and most university canteens are closed too; check the day before.

## 7. Student experiences (unofficial)

> [!NOTE]
> This section is for personal experiences; it is not a general rule or legal advice. Share yours (with a date) on [page 57](57-contribute-experiences.md) or by opening a GitHub issue.

- **Don't wait for the last day:** a semester-fee payment can take up to a week to show as received, and QIS is overloaded in the last days. Reminders in student chats usually went out a week before the deadline.
