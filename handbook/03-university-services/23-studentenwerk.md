## 1. Purpose

**Studentenwerk Hannover** is an organisation independent of the university that runs halls of residence, canteens, social counselling and BAföG. Part of your semester fee (€130) goes to it ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students at Hannover's universities.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Halls of residence:** 20 halls, online application ([LUH – Housing](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)). → [4. Finding accommodation](../01-arriving-in-hannover/04-housing.md)
2.  **Canteens:** Mensa and cafés, paid with the LeibnizCard. → [28. Mensa](28-mensa.md)
3.  **BAföG and social counselling:** BAföG, allowances, loans, scholarships and insurance; German only ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). Book social counselling: [stwh-portal.de](https://stwh-portal.de/terminreservierung-soziales/) ([Studentenwerk – Housing search](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/wohnungssuche)). ℹ️ Bring a German-speaking friend if needed.
4.  **Guide for international students:** [First Steps in Germany](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/international-students/first-steps-in-germany).
5.  **Saving money:** list of student discounts at [Save money](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/geld/geld-sparen) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [31. Shopping](../04-everyday-life/31-shopping.md)
