## 1. Purpose

The **ServiceCenter** is the first point of contact for most academic and administrative questions: enrolment, exams, student card, passwords and referral to advisors ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)). If you don't know where to go, start here.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Reception (Empfang):** handing in and collecting documents that are not sent digitally, replacing your LeibnizCard and general guidance ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
2.  **Infothek:** student assistants for basic information, applications, online services and password resets ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
3.  **Offices reachable through the desk:** Central Student Advisory Service (ZSB), Admissions Office, Examinations Office, International Office, Studentenwerk and ZQS ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
4.  **ZQS office hours at the Infothek:** learning advice Thursdays 13:00–14:00; Career Service Mondays 13:00–14:00 ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)). → [25. ZQS](25-zqs.md)
5.  **ServiceCenter or International Office?** General academic and administrative matters → ServiceCenter; visa, residence and settling in as an international student → [22. International Office](22-international-office.md).
