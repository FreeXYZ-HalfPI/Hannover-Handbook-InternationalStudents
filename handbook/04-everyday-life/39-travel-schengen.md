## 1. Purpose

With a German residence card you can make short trips to other Schengen countries. This page explains the 90/180-day rule and what to check before travelling.

## 2. Who needs this?

- 🌍 Non-EU students with a national type D visa or German residence permit.
- 🌍 EU/EEA and Swiss citizens travel freely with their ID card or passport.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Basic rule:** holders of a national residence permit of a Schengen state (and of a national type D visa) may stay in other Schengen states for up to **90 days in any 180-day period** while it is valid ([Federal Foreign Office – Schengen Agreement](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/visa-und-aufenthalt/schengen-207786)).
2.  **Calculating 90/180:** always count back 180 days from the day in question; the days of entry and exit count as days of stay ([Federal Foreign Office – Calculating the length of stay](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/fragenkatalog-node/39-berechnung-aufenthaltsdauer/606614)). The EU's short-stay calculator helps ([European Commission – Short-stay calculator](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/border-crossing/short-stay-calculator_en)).
3.  **Working in other countries:** this travel right does not include permission to work there ([Federal Foreign Office – Visa for Germany](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/fragenkatalog-node/606772-606772)).
4.  **Entry/Exit System (EES):** since late 2025 the EU registers non-EU travellers at external Schengen borders biometrically. Holders of a Schengen residence permit are exempt but should show it at border control ([European Union – EES](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/ees_en)). ⚠️
5.  **Non-Schengen countries** (e.g. the UK, Ireland, Türkiye) have their own entry rules; you may need a separate visa or electronic travel authorisation depending on your nationality. ℹ️ Check the destination's rules first.
6.  **If your permit is being renewed** and you only have a Fiktionsbescheinigung, ask the Ausländerbehörde before travelling. ⚠️ Re-entry and travel rules with a Fiktionsbescheinigung depend on its type. → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)
7.  🌍 **Travelling to your home country:** if your residence card expires while you are abroad, you will need a new visa to return ([City of Wiesbaden](https://www.wiesbaden.de/vv/produkte/33/Verlust-Aufenthaltstitel-Reiseausweis)). Stays outside Germany longer than six months can also cause a residence permit to lapse ([§ 51 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__51.html)). ℹ️ Check your card's validity before long trips.
