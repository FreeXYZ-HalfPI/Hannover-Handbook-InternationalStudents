## 1. Purpose

Even in English-taught programmes, German is needed for everyday life, official offices and work — and it greatly improves your job chances after graduation. This page summarises ways to learn and practise German in Hannover.

## 2. Who needs this?

- All international students — especially those who need **C1** or the **DSH** to enrol. Bachelor's applicants with foreign certificates need B1 to apply and C1 to enrol ([LUH – Language requirements](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/sprachnachweise)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Semester German courses** at the Leibniz Language Centre ([LLC – German](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-courses/german)) and the intensive **D-intensiv** course for the DSH. → [26. Language Centre](../03-university-services/26-language-centre.md)
2.  **Tandem:** practise with a German speaker who is learning your language ([LLC – Tandem](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-courses/tandem-learning/tandem-learning-offers)).
3.  **Self-directed learning (SDL)** at the LLC ([LLC](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
4.  **Study-Buddy** for cultural exchange ([LUH – Study-Buddy](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende/betreuungsangebote/study-buddy)).
5.  **Volkshochschule Hannover (adult education centre):** German courses and integration courses outside the university; free German-course advice without appointment on Thursdays 14:00–16:00 ([VHS Hannover – German advice](https://www.vhs-hannover.de/vhs-programm/deutsch-integration/deutschberatung-1)). Course list, including neighbourhood courses: [VHS Hannover – German courses](https://www.vhs-hannover.de/vhs-programm/deutsch-integration/deutschkurse).
6.  **Free online courses:** Deutsche Welle offers free courses from A1 to C1 ([DW – Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/overview)); the Goethe-Institut has free practice material ([Goethe-Institut – Practise German](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/ueb.html)). The Federal Employment Agency publishes a list of free websites and apps for learning German ([Federal Employment Agency – Free online German courses (PDF)](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/linksammlung-online-sprachlernangebote-deutsch_ba232254.pdf)).
7.  **Recognised certificates:** TestDaF, DSH, Goethe-Zertifikat and telc are widely accepted; check which one your programme or employer accepts ([TestDaF](https://www.testdaf.de/en/)).
    - For university admission DSH, TestDaF or telc C1 Hochschule are usually accepted; for permanent residence and many jobs, level B1 ([LUH – Language certificates](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/sprachnachweise)). → [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)

### What worked for students

8.  **Class plus daily practice:** a class alone is not enough; 20–30 minutes of listening and repeating aloud (shadowing) every day made the biggest difference. ℹ️ Student experience.
9.  **Connect German to your life:** tandem, a university sports course in German, a student job and university events. → [34. Sports and culture](34-sports-culture.md), [51. Events](../06-student-community/51-events-networking.md)
10. **Credits for language courses:** in some programmes LLC courses count towards your degree; ask your faculty about the form and conditions. ℹ️

## 7. Student experiences (unofficial)

> [!NOTE]
> This section is for personal experiences; it is not a general rule or legal advice. Share yours (with a date) on [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md) or by opening a GitHub issue.

- **LLC courses** satisfied most students; places fill quickly, registration is through Stud.IP with a placement test, and allowed absences are limited.
- **Private language schools** were more expensive for intensive courses (e.g. to go from B1 to C1 quickly); students asked for a trial lesson or others' experience first. ℹ️ This handbook does not recommend any school.
- **Speaking takes time:** several students said their speaking improved more through daily practice and real situations (work, tandem, sports club) than through classes.
- **Textbooks:** common course books (e.g. the Netzwerk series) were often swapped second-hand.
