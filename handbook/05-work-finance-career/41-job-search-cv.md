# 41. Job Search and Preparing a German CV

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 5 — Work, Finance & Career](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

CVs and applications in Germany follow their own format and conventions. This page shows where to look for jobs and where to get help writing your application.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students looking for a student job, internship or first job after graduation.

## 3. Steps

1.  **The university Career Service** helps students of all subjects with job search, applications, internships and contacts with employers ([ZQS – Career Service](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc/career-service)). → [45. Career service](45-career-service.md)
2.  **Individual advice** on CVs and applications: [Individual advice](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc/career-guidance-and-service/individual-advice).
3.  **Programme for international students** at the Career Service: [Internationals welcome – Students](https://www.zqs.uni-hannover.de/en/kc/career-service/internationals-welcome/students).
4.  **Career Dates job fair** ([LUH – ZQS](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/organisation/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/zqs)).
5.  **Career Service office hour at the Infothek:** Mondays 13:00–14:00 ([ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
6.  **Job portals:** the Federal Employment Agency's job exchange ([BA – Jobsuche](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/)) and the official portal for international professionals ([Make it in Germany – Job listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-listings)).
7.  **German CV (Lebenslauf):** usually tabular and in reverse chronological order, one to two pages, sent with a cover letter (Anschreiben) and relevant certificates. A photo and date of birth are common but not required — employers may not discriminate on such grounds (General Equal Treatment Act). ℹ️ Sample templates: [Europass CV](https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv).
8.  🌍 **Foreign degrees and professions:** for regulated professions (e.g. teaching, medicine, some engineering titles) you need formal recognition; the "Recognition Finder" tells you what applies ([Recognition in Germany – Recognition Finder](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/index.php)).
