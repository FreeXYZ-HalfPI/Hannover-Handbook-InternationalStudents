# 36. Waste Separation, House Rules and Living in Germany

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 4 — Everyday Life in Hannover](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

In Germany waste must be separated and buildings have house rules (Hausordnung). Ignoring them is the most common cause of trouble with neighbours and landlords.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** residents of the city and region of Hannover.

## 3. Steps

**Waste separation in the Hannover region**

1.  **Yellow bin (Gelbe Tonne):** lightweight packaging (plastic, metal, cartons); it replaced the yellow bag and is free for residents ([hannover.de – Yellow bin](https://www.hannover.de/Service/Presse-Medien/Hannover.de/Aktuelles/Service-2022/Aha-f%C3%BChrt-Gelbe-Tonne-ein)). In the city, aha is responsible; outside the city, Remondis ([hannover.de – Yellow bag](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/Umwelt-Nachhaltigkeit/Abfall-Bodenschutz/Abfallentsorgung-und-%C3%BCberwachung/Entsorgung-von-Leichtverpackungen-Gelber-Sack)).
2.  **Organic bin (Biotonne):** replaced organic-waste bags across the region on 1 August 2024. Plastic (even "bioplastic"), **cooked food**, nappies and pet litter are not allowed ([hannover.de](https://www.hannover.de/Service/Presse-Medien/Hannover.de/Aktuelles/Service-2025/Gelbe-Tonne,-Rest%C2%ADm%C3%BCll%C2%ADtonne-und-Alt%C2%ADpapier%C2%ADtonne)).
3.  **Residual waste (Restabfalltonne):** bins have replaced bags since early 2024 ([hannover.de](https://www.hannover.de/Service/Presse-Medien/Hannover.de/Aktuelles/Service-2025/Gelbe-Tonne,-Rest%C2%ADm%C3%BCll%C2%ADtonne-und-Alt%C2%ADpapier%C2%ADtonne)).
4.  **Paper (Altpapiertonne):** since August 2025 collected only in paper bins ([hannover.de](https://www.hannover.de/Service/Presse-Medien/Hannover.de/Aktuelles/Service-2025/Gelbe-Tonne,-Rest%C2%ADm%C3%BCll%C2%ADtonne-und-Alt%C2%ADpapier%C2%ADtonne)).
5.  **Glass:** separate by colour (white, brown, green) into glass containers ([aha – Glass](https://www.aha-region.de/abfaelle-und-wertstoffe/altglas)). ℹ️ Avoid using them at night and on Sundays (noise).
6.  **Bulky waste (Sperrmüll):** collection of up to 5 m³ is free by appointment; each item max. 2 m long and 75 kg. Put items out on collection day by 6:00, not earlier ([aha – Bulky waste collection](https://www.aha-region.de/abfaelle-und-wertstoffe/sperrabfaelle/sperrabfallabholung)). You can also take up to 1 m³ per day free to a **recycling centre (Wertstoffhof)**.
7.  **Collection calendar** for your address: [aha-region.de](https://www.aha-region.de/). The aha app also sends reminders.

**House rules**

1.  Get the **house rules (Hausordnung)** from your landlord and read them (stairwell cleaning, quiet hours, etc.). ℹ️
2.  **Quiet hours (Ruhezeiten)** usually apply at night (often 22:00–06:00) and on Sundays and public holidays; details are in the Hausordnung. ℹ️
3.  **Air regularly (Lüften)** — open windows wide for a few minutes several times a day — to prevent mould. ℹ️
4.  **Heating and electricity** are often billed annually; a large back-payment can follow if you heat a lot. ℹ️ Check your Nebenkosten statement.

## 5. Responsible office

- **Official name:** Zweckverband Abfallwirtschaft Region Hannover (aha), the region's waste management authority
- **Contact:** free hotline **0800 999 11 99** (Mon–Thu 7:00–16:30, Fri 7:00–15:00) ([aha](https://www.aha-region.de/abfaelle-und-wertstoffe/sperrabfaelle/sperrabfallabholung))
