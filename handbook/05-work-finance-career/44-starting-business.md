## 1. Purpose

If you have a business or start-up idea, the university and the city of Hannover offer free advice, workshops and funding routes such as the EXIST grant.

## 2. Who needs this?

- LUH students, researchers and graduates with a business idea or who want to join start-up teams.

## 3. Steps

1.  **starting business:** a joint service of **hannoverimpuls** (the economic development agency of the city and region) and LUH that helps turn innovative ideas into business plans; advice is free ([starting-business.de](https://starting-business.de/); [Free advice](https://starting-business.de/beratung); [LUH – Business start-ups](https://www.uni-hannover.de/forschung/transfer/existenzgruendung)).
2.  People not affiliated with the university are referred to hannoverimpuls ([starting-business.de – Advice](https://starting-business.de/beratung)).
3.  **University patents and start-ups office:** SG 44 – Patente und Gründung ([LUH – SG 44](https://www.uni-hannover.de/universitaet/organisation/dezernate/dezernat-4/sg-44-geistiges-eigentum-schutzrechte-gruendung)).
4.  **Doctoral candidates and researchers:** [Self-employment / Starting a business – Graduate Academy](https://www.graduiertenakademie.uni-hannover.de/en/career/career-areas/self-employment-starting-a-business).
5.  **Federal platform:** [Gründerplattform](https://gruenderplattform.de/), a free online guide from planning to financing.
6.  🌍 **Residence:** self-employment after graduation may require a residence permit for self-employment (§ 21 AufenthG); graduates of German universities have eased conditions ([§ 21 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__21.html); [Make it in Germany – Self-employment](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/self-employment)). → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md), [46. After graduation](46-after-graduation.md)
