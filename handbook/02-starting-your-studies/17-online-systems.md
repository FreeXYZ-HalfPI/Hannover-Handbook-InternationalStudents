# 17. University Systems, E-mail and Learning Platforms

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 2 — Starting Your Studies](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

The university has several online systems whose names confuse newcomers. This page explains what each one is for and how to log in.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  Your **LUH-ID and initial password** arrive by post with the LeibnizCard; keep them safe ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
2.  **IdM (identity management):** the central login for activating and managing your Wi-Fi, cloud storage and learning-platform accounts. Log in the first time with your LUH-ID and initial password ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist); [activation guide](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/online-dienste/idm#c60368)).
3.  **University e-mail:** official messages (exam changes, fees, deadlines) go to your university address. Check it regularly or forward it. ℹ️
4.  **LUH-WLAN / eduroam:** wireless internet in almost all buildings ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). IT services: [LUIS](https://www.luis.uni-hannover.de/).
5.  **Stud.IP:** online courses, files and contact with lecturers; registration for some courses and language courses happens here ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
6.  **QIS portal:** enrolment certificate, semester-fee status, course list and exam registration ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist); [LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).
7.  **RIDE:** get your semester ticket with your Stud.IP login ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)).
8.  **LUH study-start app:** website content, the "Noch Fragen?" brochure, preparatory courses, canteen menus and more ([LUH – Mobile app](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/mobile-app-zum-studienstart)).
9.  **Forgot your password?** Student assistants at the Infothek in the ServiceCenter can reset it ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [21. ServiceCenter](../03-university-services/21-servicecenter.md)
10. **Software and VPN:** students get free or discounted software and a VPN for off-campus access to licensed resources through LUIS. ℹ️
11. Full list of digital tools: [Online services](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/online-dienste).
