## 1. Purpose

Students from some countries face extra hurdles: international bank transfers may be blocked or delayed because of EU or international sanctions, capital controls in their home country, or banks' risk policies. This page explains what to expect and where to get help — without singling out any country.

## 2. Who needs this?

- 🌍 Students whose home country is subject to **EU sanctions** (you can check this on the EU Sanctions Map) or whose government restricts money transfers abroad ([EU Sanctions Map](https://www.sanctionsmap.eu/)).
- Students whose family cannot easily send money to Germany.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Know the rules:** EU sanctions are mostly targeted at specific persons, companies, banks and goods — they do **not** forbid you from studying in Germany or from having a bank account here. However, transfers from sanctioned banks may be blocked, and German banks must carry out extra checks ([Deutsche Bundesbank – Financial sanctions](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/service/financial-sanctions)).
2.  **Plan your finances early:** transferring money for a blocked account can take weeks or be impossible from some countries. Ask blocked-account providers **before paying** whether they accept transfers from your country and in which currency. Alternatives accepted by German missions: a **Verpflichtungserklärung** (formal obligation letter) by someone living in Germany, or a scholarship ([Federal Foreign Office (German Embassy Managua) – Blocked account](https://managua.diplo.de/ni-de/service/05-visaeinreise/sperrkonto-2163974)). → [2. Before you arrive](../01-arriving-in-hannover/02-before-arrival-visa.md)
3.  **Opening a German account:** you have a legal right to a **basic payment account (Basiskonto)**, regardless of nationality ([§ 31 ZKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zkg/__31.html); [BaFin](https://www.bafin.de/EN/Homepage/homepage_node.html)). A bank may ask questions about the source of your money — keep documents (scholarship letters, family transfer receipts) ready. → [6. Bank account](../01-arriving-in-hannover/06-bank-account.md)
4.  **Use legal channels only:** informal money-transfer systems or carrying large amounts of cash create problems. Cash over **€10,000** must be declared to customs when entering the EU ([German Customs – Cash controls](https://www.zoll.de/EN/Private-individuals/Travel/Entering-Germany/Restrictions/Cash/cash_node.html)).
5.  **Semester fee and rent:** pay from a German account to avoid rejected transfers. If money is stuck, tell the university **before** the deadline — ask the International Office for advice. → [13. Semester fee](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
6.  **Emergency financial help:** if a sudden crisis in your home country cuts off your funding, the International Office's emergency aid and other funds may help. → [43. Scholarships and financial support](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
7.  **Online services and software:** some services (e.g. app stores, cloud or payment services) restrict access for users from sanctioned countries. With a German address and account this is usually solved. ℹ️
8.  **Export control in research:** certain research areas (e.g. some engineering, physics or IT fields) are subject to export-control rules, which can affect access to labs or projects for nationals of some countries. Your institute is responsible for checking this ([BAFA – Export control](https://www.bafa.de/EN/Foreign_Trade/Export_Control/export_control_node.html)). ⚠️
9.  **Consular services:** if your country's consulates in Germany have closed or have limited services (passport renewal, document legalisation), contact your embassy in Berlin early — passport renewals can take a long time. Your residence permit depends on a valid passport. → [64. Religion, food and home communities](64-religion-food-communities.md)
10. **Talk to someone:** the Welcome Centre and AStA know these situations well and can refer you to specialist advice. → [22. International Office](../03-university-services/22-international-office.md), [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)

## 4. Documents and costs

| Item | Required / possible | Notes | Source |
|----|----|----|----|
| Proof of origin of funds | Possible | Banks may ask | [Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/service/financial-sanctions) |
| Verpflichtungserklärung | Possible | Alternative to a blocked account | [Federal Foreign Office](https://managua.diplo.de/ni-de/service/05-visaeinreise/sperrkonto-2163974) |
| Customs declaration for cash ≥ €10,000 | Required if applicable | — | [German Customs](https://www.zoll.de/EN/Private-individuals/Travel/Entering-Germany/Restrictions/Cash/cash_node.html) |

## 5. Responsible office

- **Sanctions questions (financial):** Deutsche Bundesbank, Financial Sanctions Service Centre ([bundesbank.de](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/service/financial-sanctions))
- **Bank account complaints:** BaFin ([bafin.de](https://www.bafin.de/EN/Homepage/homepage_node.html))
- **University advice:** Welcome Centre — incoming-student@uni-hannover.de
