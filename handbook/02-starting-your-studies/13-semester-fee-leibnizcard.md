# 13. Semester Fee and Your Student Card (LeibnizCard)

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 2 — Starting Your Studies](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

Every semester you must pay the **semester fee (Semesterbeitrag)** to renew your enrolment (Rückmeldung); otherwise you are de-registered under Lower Saxony's Higher Education Act ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)). The **LeibnizCard** is your student ID, library card, sports card, canteen payment card and locker key in one ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students, every semester.
- 🌍 **No tuition fees** for regular degree students in Lower Saxony — the same for EU and non-EU students. Only the semester fee (and, after a long study period, the long-term fee below) applies ([LUH – Study costs](https://www.uni-hannover.de/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/studienkosten)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **First semester:** use the payment reference (Verwendungszweck) stated in your admission letter ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).
2.  **Later semesters:** log in to the portal with your IdM username and check "My Account Balance" for the amount and reference ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).
3.  Transfer the full amount to: Stiftung Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Universität Hannover · IBAN: DE51 2505 0000 0151 8129 06 · BIC: NOLADE2HXXX ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).
4.  **Payment reference for winter 2026/27:** for 7-digit matriculation numbers "26200" + number + "7"; for 8-digit numbers "2620" + number + "7" ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)). ℹ️ The prefix changes every semester.
5.  It can take up to a week for the payment status to show "Cleared" ([LUH – Re-registration](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/organising-your-studies/rueckmeldung)).
6.  **Upload your photo** before enrolment at [photo.idm.uni-hannover.de](https://photo.idm.uni-hannover.de/en/students/). The LeibnizCard arrives by post from mid-September (winter) or March (summer), together with your LUH-ID and initial password ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
7.  The card is pre-validated for the first semester; from the second semester you must **re-validate** it at the validation terminals ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
8.  Print your **enrolment certificate (Immatrikulationsbescheinigung)** from the [QIS portal](https://qis.verwaltung.uni-hannover.de/) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
9.  Get your **semester ticket (Deutschlandsemesterticket)** through the RIDE portal with your Stud.IP login ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). → [30. Public transport](../04-everyday-life/30-public-transport.md)
10. **Financial hardship?** AStA can refund the semester-ticket part in social hardship cases, and Studentenwerk/LUH offer emergency aid ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). → [29. Counselling](../03-university-services/29-counselling.md), [43. Scholarships](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md) ⚠️ Check conditions and deadlines.

**Related pages:** [19. Leave, change of programme and withdrawal](19-leave-change-withdrawal.md)
