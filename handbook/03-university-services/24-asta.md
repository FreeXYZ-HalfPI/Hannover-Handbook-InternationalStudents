# 24. General Students' Committee — AStA

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 3 — University Services](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

The **AStA (Allgemeiner Studierendenausschuss)** is the executive committee of the student body. For international students it offers first-level advice on studies, residence permits, student jobs, finances and employment rights, and it runs the semester ticket ([AStA – International students](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende); [AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students; the international department is specifically for international students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Open advice (Erstberatung & Orientierung):** no appointment needed during office hours; topics: studies, residence permit, student jobs, finances and employment law ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)).
2.  **Referral:** AStA refers you to the right specialist advice centres in Hannover ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)).
3.  **Support for international student groups** and the foreign students' commission (AusländerInnenkommission) ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)). → [49. International student groups](../06-student-community/49-international-groups.md)
4.  **Financial help:** short-term AStA loans (Darlehen) and the subsidised canteen meal card (Mensafreitisch) ([LUH – Financial aid for international students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)). → [43. Scholarships and funding](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
5.  **Semester ticket:** for ticket problems e-mail semesterticket@asta-hannover.de ([AStA – Semester ticket](https://www.asta-hannover.de/semesterticket)). → [30. Public transport](../04-everyday-life/30-public-transport.md)
6.  The AStA is elected by the **Student Council (Studentischer Rat)**. → [52. Student representation](../06-student-community/52-student-representation.md)
