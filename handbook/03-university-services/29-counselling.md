## 1. Purpose

This page lists free counselling for **academic, psychological, social and financial** problems and tells you where to go for each.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students — especially new international students in their first months. Homesickness, loneliness and culture shock are common and nothing to be ashamed of.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Academic advice** (choosing or changing programme, study problems): Zentrale Studienberatung — studienberatung@uni-hannover.de · +49 511 762 5580 ([LUH – ServiceCenter](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/beratung-hilfe/servicecenter)).
2.  **Psychological counselling (ptb):** free, confidential and anonymous on request; for exam anxiety, learning problems, relationship issues, loneliness with depression or suicidal thoughts, and **adjusting to a new country and culture**. Counselling is also available in English ([ptb](https://www.ptb.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
3.  **First ptb appointment** is booked by phone ([TiHo – ptb](https://www.tiho-hannover.de/en/studies-education/general-information-for-students/counselling-and-support-services/psychological-therapeutic-counselling)). You can fill in the questionnaire and consent form before the first session ([ptb](https://www.ptb.uni-hannover.de/en/)).
4.  **Psychotherapy outside the university:** with statutory health insurance you can see a psychotherapist without a referral; the appointment service **116 117** helps find an initial consultation ([116117 – Appointment service](https://www.116117.de/de/terminservice.php)). ℹ️ Therapists who work in your language can be searched for in the Lower Saxony psychotherapists' directory ([Psychotherapeutenkammer Niedersachsen – Therapist search](https://www.pknds.de/)).
5.  **Social and financial advice:** BAföG and social counselling at Studentenwerk (in German) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [23. Studentenwerk](23-studentenwerk.md)
6.  **Financial advice for international students:** International Office (emergency aid); the church organisations KED (Protestant) and KHG (Catholic) also offer advice and emergency aid, regardless of religion ([LUH – Financial aid for international students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)). → [43. Scholarships and funding](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
7.  **Residence, work and employment rights:** AStA's open advice ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)). → [24. AStA](24-asta.md)
8.  **Discrimination and diversity** → [27. ChancenVielfalt](27-chancenvielfalt.md)
9.  **Study methods** → [25. ZQS](25-zqs.md)
