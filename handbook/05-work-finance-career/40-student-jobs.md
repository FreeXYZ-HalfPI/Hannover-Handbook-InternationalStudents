## 1. Purpose

This page explains the types of student jobs (HiWi, Werkstudent, Minijob) and the **legal limits** on working as a student; breaking these limits can put your residence permit at risk.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.
- 🌍 **EU/EEA and Swiss students** may work without residence-law limits, like German students (social-insurance rules still apply) ([Make it in Germany – Working while studying](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-vocational-training/studying-in-germany/work)).
- 🌍 **Non-EU students** with a study permit are limited as described below. Refugees and people with temporary protection have different rules → [63. Refugees and protection status](../08-by-nationality/63-refugees-protection-status.md).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Limit for non-EU students:** without approval from the Federal Employment Agency, at most **140 working days per year**, tracked as a working-day account ([§ 16b (3) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html); [Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-vocational-training/studying-in-germany/work)).
2.  **How days are counted:** whichever method is more favourable for you, week by week: (a) a day with up to 4 hours of work counts as half a day, longer days as full days; or (b) up to **20 hours per week during the lecture period**, and any number of hours per week **during semester breaks**, count as **2.5 working days** ([§ 16b (3) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html)).
3.  **Student side jobs (studentische Nebentätigkeiten)** — e.g. HiWi jobs at the university — do not count towards the 140 days ([§ 16b (3) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html)). ℹ️ Ask the Ausländerbehörde whether your specific job qualifies.
4.  **Internships:** a compulsory internship in your curriculum counts as part of your studies; a voluntary internship outside it counts towards the limit or needs approval from the Federal Employment Agency (BA) ([BA – Technical instructions (PDF)](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/dok_ba033210.pdf)). → [45. Career service and internships](45-career-service.md)
5.  **Self-employment/freelancing** is not covered by the 140 days and needs separate permission from the Ausländerbehörde. ⚠️
6.  **Types of jobs:**
    - **HiWi (studentische Hilfskraft):** student assistant at a university institute; ads on institute websites and the [university job page](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/stellenangebote-arbeit-an-der-uni).
    - **Werkstudent:** subject-related work at companies, usually at most 20 hours per week during the semester.
    - **Minijob:** earnings up to **€603 per month** in 2026 (€7,236 per year) ([Deutsche Rentenversicherung](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Experten/Arbeitgeber-und-Steuerberater/summa-summarum/Lexikon/W/werkstudentenprivileg.html); [iww.de](https://www.iww.de/pp/recht/geringfuegige-beschaeftigung-minijob-2026-hoeherer-verdienst-neue-regeln-und-was-sonst-zu-beachten-ist-f171358)).
7.  **Minimum wage:** €13.90 per hour in 2026, €14.60 from 1 January 2027 ([Federal Government – Minimum wage](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/mindestlohn-steigt-2365658)). This applies to everyone regardless of nationality.
8.  **Social security number (Sozialversicherungsnummer):** issued automatically and free of charge with your first job subject to social insurance ([Deutsche Rentenversicherung](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Ueber-uns-und-Presse/Presse/Meldungen/2024/240312-versicherungsnummernachweis)).
9.  **Work is not enough to finance your studies:** the Ausländerbehörde expects your livelihood to be secured independently of a job. ℹ️
10. **Tax, insurance and payslips** → [42. Tax and payslips](42-tax-payslip.md)
11. **Employment-law advice** for international students: AStA ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)); the DGB's "Faire Mobilität" also advises foreign workers in many languages ([Faire Mobilität](https://www.faire-mobilitaet.de/en)). → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)
