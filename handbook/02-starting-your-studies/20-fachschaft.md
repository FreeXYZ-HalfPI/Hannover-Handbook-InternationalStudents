## 1. Purpose

The **Fachschaft** (subject student council) is made up of senior students in your programme and answers questions about your subject ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). It is usually the first and friendliest place to ask.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students, especially in the first semester. International students are welcome — many Fachschaften also run events in English.

## 3. Steps

1.  Find your Fachschaft or Fachrat in the [list of Fachschaften and Fachräte](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/organe-gremien/fachschaften) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
2.  Join your programme's orientation events (O-Woche); every programme has one ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). ℹ️ These are often organised with the Fachschaft.
3.  Visit the Fachschaft's office hours with questions about the curriculum, exams and experience with courses; many also collect old exams for practice. ℹ️
4.  You can become an active member or representative; Fachschaft councils (Fachschaftsräte) are elected — international students can vote and stand for election. → [52. Student representation](../06-student-community/52-student-representation.md)
