## 1. Purpose

This page gives a **practical order** for the tasks of your first two weeks. The details of each task are on its own page; this page only tells you **when** and **in which order** to do them.

## 2. Who needs this?

- New arrivals who will live in Hannover and have not yet done any of the administrative tasks.
- Students who started this semester and may have missed one of the steps.

🌍 Which tasks apply to you depends on your nationality: EU/EEA and Swiss citizens, for example, do not need a visa or a study residence permit. → [Section 8 — Information by nationality](../08-by-nationality/README.md)

## 3. Steps

### Phase A — before you arrive

- ☐ **Complete your pre-departure documents:** national visa (type D) if you need one, blocked account and travel insurance. → [2. Before you arrive](02-before-arrival-visa.md)
- ☐ **Arrange where you will sleep in the first week** (hall, temporary room or shared flat). → [4. Finding accommodation](04-housing.md)
- ☐ **Carry the originals of your main documents:** passport, visa, admission letter and translated certificates. → [12. Admission and enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md)

### Phase B — days 1 to 3 (arriving and settling in)

- ☐ **Get from the airport or station to your accommodation** and buy the right ticket. Your semester ticket is not valid yet if the semester has not started. → [3. Arriving in Hannover](03-arrival-transport.md), [30. Public transport](../04-everyday-life/30-public-transport.md)
- ☐ **Buy a SIM card** so the bank, insurer and offices can reach you. → [8. SIM card and internet](08-sim-internet.md)
- ☐ **Keep your rental contract and deposit receipt** and take photos of them. → [4. Finding accommodation](04-housing.md)

### Phase C — days 3 to 10 (offices and bank)

- ☐ **Register your address (Anmeldung) at the Bürgeramt.** The deadline is at most **two weeks** after you move in, and you need the landlord's confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung). → [5. Registering your address](05-anmeldung.md)
- ☐ **Open a bank account.** The registration certificate is usually needed. → [6. Opening a bank account](06-bank-account.md)
- ☐ **Choose and register your health insurance.** Do this as soon as possible after enrolling. → [7. Health insurance](07-health-insurance.md)
- ☐ **Prepare your residence permit application** (if you need one). When you have the registration certificate and the account, submit it without delay. → [10. Residence permit](10-residence-permit.md)

### Phase D — days 10 to 14 (university and semester matters)

- ☐ **Complete final enrolment (Immatrikulation) and pay the semester fee.** → [12. Admission and enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md), [13. Semester fee and student card](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
- ☐ **Follow up your Tax ID (Steuer-ID).** The letter usually arrives a few weeks later. → [9. Tax ID and official letters](09-tax-id-broadcasting-fee.md)
- ☐ **Keep your official mail in one folder:** photograph every letter from an office or the university and store the photos together.

## 4. Documents to keep in one permanent folder

ℹ️ This list is for quick reference; always ask each office what it needs.

- Passport and visa; keep copies separately.
- Rental contract and landlord's confirmation.
- Registration certificate (after it is issued).
- Admission letter and enrolment certificate.
- Health insurance certificate.
- Bank account and card.

## 5. If a step is behind

- **The two-week registration deadline has passed?** Go to the Bürgeramt now and explain your move-in date. ℹ️ Penalties and conditions are on page 5.
- **Your residence permit is close to expiry?** Apply for the extension before it expires so you receive a provisional certificate (Fiktionsbescheinigung). → [10. Residence permit](10-residence-permit.md)
- **Insurance or account not active yet?** Ask the provider before paying anything and keep the receipt.
