## 1. Purpose

The **Mensa** (student canteen) is the cheapest way to get a hot lunch at university. This page shows where the canteens are, how to pay and how to find food that fits your diet.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Payment:** pay cashless with your LeibnizCard; no separate validation is needed ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). Keep the card on the reader until payment is complete ([Studentenwerk – Canteen FAQ](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/food/canteen-faq)).
2.  **Topping up:** at top-up machines, €5–70 with €5, €10, €20 and €50 notes; in some canteens by bank card. Direct payment by bank card or phone is only possible at Contine, TiHo-Tower and Garbsen ([Studentenwerk – Canteen FAQ](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/food/canteen-faq)).
3.  **Studentenwerk canteens:** Hauptmensa, Contine, Mensa Garbsen, Mensa Campus Linden, Mensa Herrenhausen, Mensa Blumhardtstraße, Mensa Große Pause and more ([Studentenwerk – Canteens](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/food/canteens-cafeterias)).
4.  **Cafés:** Café Leibniz in the Hauptmensa, Café-bar in the Contine, Moccabar, Coffee-Shop Appelstraße, Cafeteria Sprengelstube and more ([Studentenwerk – Canteens](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/food/canteens-cafeterias)).
5.  **Opening times:** [Opening times at a glance](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/food/opening-times-at-a-glance).
6.  **Daily menu** is also in the LUH study-start app ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [17. Online systems](../02-starting-your-studies/17-online-systems.md)
7.  **Dietary needs:** menus mark vegetarian and vegan dishes and list allergens and additives; dishes containing pork or alcohol are labelled. ⚠️ Halal certification is not generally indicated — check the labels or ask staff. → [64. Religion, food and home communities](../08-by-nationality/64-religion-food-communities.md)
8.  **Help for students on a low income:** AStA's subsidised meal card (Mensafreitisch) ([LUH – Financial aid for international students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)). → [43. Scholarships and funding](../05-work-finance-career/43-scholarships.md)
