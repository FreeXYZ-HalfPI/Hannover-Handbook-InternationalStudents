# Living in Hannover — A Practical Handbook for International Students

> [!TIP]
> **A step-by-step guide for international students of Leibniz University Hannover (LUH) — from every country.** · Whole handbook last reviewed: 2026-10-24

## What is this handbook?

A practical, step-by-step guide for international students at Leibniz University Hannover (LUH): from applying while still at home, to arriving in Hannover, studying, working and staying after graduation. Each topic has its own page, and every page answers the same questions: What do I need to do? Where do I start? Which documents do I need and what does it cost? Which office is responsible? Where can I find official, up-to-date information?

Most pages apply to **every** international student. Where rules differ by nationality or status, you'll see the 🌍 symbol — and **[Section 8](08-by-nationality/README.md)** collects everything that depends on where you come from.

## How every page is structured

1.  **Purpose:** what problem the page solves.
2.  **Who needs this?** All students, international students, or a specific group (🌍).
3.  **Steps:** in order, in plain language.
4.  **Documents and costs:** separating required from possible items.
5.  **Responsible office:** official name, address and contact.
6.  **Sources and official links:** a table of every source on the page with its **access date** and check result.
7.  **Student experiences:** kept separate from official information; not legal advice.

The last review date of each page is in the green box at the top.

## Symbols

| Symbol | Meaning |
|------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| 🌍 | Depends on your nationality or residence status |
| ⚠️ | Information needs confirmation from an official source |
| ℹ️ | General tip or reminder |
| Yellow box | Important warning or "not legal advice" reminder |
| "unofficial / secondary source" | A figure or claim found only in a non-official source |
| ✅ in the sources table | Link opened on the access date |

## Contributing and maintenance

- **Report an error or add an experience:** → [57. Suggestions and experiences](07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md)
- **Source rule:** every claim, figure, deadline or fee needs a source link next to it and must appear in the page's sources table with an access date. Priority: the university (uni-hannover.de), the City and Region of Hannover (hannover.de), the law (gesetze-im-internet.de), the Federal Government's portal (make-it-in-germany.com) and the responsible office.
- **Updates:** residence, insurance, tax and work rules change quickly; review these pages at least once a semester and update the dates.

> [!WARNING]
> This handbook is general guidance, not legal, tax or medical advice. The responsible official office always has the final word.
