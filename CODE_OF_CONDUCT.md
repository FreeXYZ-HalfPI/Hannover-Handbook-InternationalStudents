# Code of Conduct

This handbook exists to help new students. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, comments on the website and in the ISL channel — agrees to:

- **Be respectful.** No insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, whatever someone's nationality, religion, gender, origin or residence status.
- **Stay on topic and be honest.** Share experience and corrections that help students; say when you are unsure; no advertising or spam.
- **Protect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal data, and do not out anyone's residence or military-service status.
- **Disagree with sources, not people.** If you think a page is wrong, say what the official source says.

**What happens if the rules are broken:** the maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions, lock a discussion and block accounts. Serious cases (threats, doxxing) are reported to GitHub.

Rules for the website comment box: [COMMENTS.md](COMMENTS.md).

**Report a problem:** open an [issue](https://github.com/FreeXYZ-HalfPI/Hannover-Handbook-InternationalStudents/issues/new/choose) or write in the [ISL Telegram channel](https://t.me/hannover_iranian_students). Reports about a comment can also use the "Report" function on GitHub.
