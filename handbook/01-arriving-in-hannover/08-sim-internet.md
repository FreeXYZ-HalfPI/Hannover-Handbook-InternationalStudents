## 1. Purpose

A German phone number and internet are needed in the first days for appointments, the bank, university e-mail and contacting your landlord. This page covers **SIM cards and internet for the first days**; long-term contracts are in [32. Internet, mobile and service contracts](../04-everyday-life/32-contracts.md).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** newcomers.
- 🌍 **EU/EEA SIM cards** work in Germany without roaming charges under "roam like at home" — but only for periodic travel; operators may charge extra if you live in Germany permanently ([Your Europe – Roaming](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/internet-telecoms/mobile-roaming-costs/index_en.htm)).

## 3. Steps

1.  **Prepaid SIM cards** are sold in shops and online; your identity must be verified before activation ([§ 172 TKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tkg_2021/__172.html)).
2.  **ID check is mandatory:** the operator must verify your name, address and date of birth with an ID document before activation. Allowed methods (in person, PostIdent or video identification) are set by the Bundesnetzagentur ([Bundesnetzagentur – ID procedures for prepaid](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/tkg-identverfahren.html)). ℹ️ Some video-ident systems do not accept every passport; in-shop activation is the fallback.
3.  **University internet:** connect to **LUH-WLAN** (or **eduroam**, which also works at universities worldwide) with your LUH-ID; it is available in almost all buildings and on campus ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [17. Online systems](../02-starting-your-studies/17-online-systems.md)
4.  Many Studentenwerk halls have fast and cheap internet ([LUH – Housing](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/campus-und-stadt/housing)).
5.  Some mobile providers offer **student discounts** on presentation of an enrolment certificate ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
6.  **Free Wi-Fi in town:** many trains, stations and cafés offer Wi-Fi. ℹ️ Use a VPN or avoid banking on open networks.
