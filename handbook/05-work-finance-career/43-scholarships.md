## 1. Purpose

This page lists financial aid and scholarships that students — especially international students — in Hannover can apply for, from emergency aid to long-term scholarships.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students; some aid is only for non-EU students.
- 🌍 **BAföG** (state student finance) is usually only for German students, but some international students qualify — e.g. EU citizens who have lived/worked in Germany long enough, recognised refugees and some people with long-term residence ([§ 8 BAföG – eligible foreign nationals](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/baf_g/__8.html); [LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). ⚠️ Ask Studentenwerk's BAföG office.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Emergency aid (Notfall-Beihilfe) from the International Office:** for sudden financial crises not caused by you, e.g. theft, fire, sudden job loss or illness. The amount is not fixed, it is usually paid only once during your studies and there is no legal right to it ([LUH – Financial aid for international students](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)).
2.  **Degree-completion aid (Studienabschlussbeihilfe):** for non-EU students with a study residence permit in their thesis phase; partial monthly payments. Application periods each year: **1–31 January, 1–31 March, 1–31 July and 1–31 October**, followed by a 20–30-minute interview ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)).
3.  **AStA:** short-term loans (Darlehen) and the subsidised canteen card (Mensafreitisch) ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)). → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)
4.  **KED and KHG (church organisations):** advice and emergency financial aid, regardless of religion ([LUH](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/finanzielle-beihilfen-fuer-internationale-studierende)).
5.  **Deutschlandstipendium:** €300 per month for two semesters, for German and international students of all subjects. 2026/27 round: applications **10 August – 30 September 2026** via the university's online portal ([LUH – Deutschlandstipendium news](https://www.uni-hannover.de/universitaet/aktuelles/online-aktuell/details/news/bewerbungsphase-fuer-deutschlandstipendien-laeuft-1); [LUH – Deutschlandstipendium](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/deutschlandstipendium)). ℹ️ Check the next deadline around August 2027.
6.  **DAAD scholarship database:** search by country of origin, subject and degree level ([DAAD – Scholarship database](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/)). 🌍 Many DAAD programmes are country-specific — apply from your home country, often a year in advance.
7.  **Political and religious foundations** (Begabtenförderungswerke) also fund international students, e.g. Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung, Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung, Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung, Rosa-Luxemburg-Stiftung, Evangelisches Studienwerk, Cusanuswerk and the Avicenna-Studienwerk for Muslim students ([Stipendium Plus – the foundations](https://www.stipendiumplus.de/)).
8.  🌍 **Home-country scholarships:** many governments fund study in Germany (ask your ministry of education or the German embassy).

- **Contact:** incoming-student@uni-hannover.de · +49 511 762 3964; an in-person consultation before applying is recommended.
