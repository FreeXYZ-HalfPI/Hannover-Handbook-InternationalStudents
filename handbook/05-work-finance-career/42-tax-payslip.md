## 1. Purpose

When you work, you receive a payslip (Gehaltsabrechnung) showing deductions for tax and insurance. This page explains the basics and how to get tax back with a tax return.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Students with a job. → [40. Student jobs](40-student-jobs.md)

## 3. Steps

1.  Give your **tax ID (Steuer-ID)** to your employer. → [9. Tax ID](../01-arriving-in-hannover/09-tax-id-broadcasting-fee.md)
2.  **Basic tax-free allowance (Grundfreibetrag) 2026:** €12,348 per year; income up to this amount is tax-free ([Federal Ministry of Finance – Monthly report Feb 2026](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Monatsberichte/Ausgabe/2026/02/Inhalte/Kapitel-2-Analysen/2-5-wichtigste-steuerliche-aenderungen-2026.html)).
3.  **Minijobs** up to €603 per month have simpler rules. → [40. Student jobs](40-student-jobs.md)
4.  **Insurance in student jobs:** working up to **20 hours a week** during the semester, you are exempt from health, long-term care and unemployment insurance, but pay **pension insurance** on earnings over €603 per month. Working more than 20 hours (except in semester breaks, or evenings/weekends for up to 26 weeks) means all insurances apply ([DRV – Student employee privilege](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Experten/Arbeitgeber-und-Steuerberater/summa-summarum/Lexikon/W/werkstudentenprivileg.html)).
5.  **Tax return (Steuererklärung):** if tax was deducted during the year but your annual income was below the allowance, you can usually get it back — up to four years retroactively. Official free system: [ELSTER](https://www.elster.de/) (English interface available). ℹ️ For your own case, consult a tax adviser or a wage-tax assistance association (Lohnsteuerhilfeverein).
6.  🌍 **Double taxation agreements:** Germany has treaties with many countries; some contain special rules for students (e.g. on payments from home). Check the treaty with your country ([Federal Ministry of Finance – Double taxation agreements](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Web/DE/Themen/Steuern/Internationales_Steuerrecht/Staatenbezogene_Informationen/staatenbezogene_info.html)). ⚠️
7.  **Church tax (Kirchensteuer):** may be deducted depending on the religion you declared at registration ([LUH – Entry and Residence](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/universitaet/internationales/wege-nach-hannover/einreise-aufenthalt)). → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)

## 5. Responsible office

- **Official name:** the tax office (Finanzamt) for your address. ⚠️ Find the office for your district on the Lower Saxony tax administration website ([Steuerverwaltung Niedersachsen – Finanzämter](https://lstn.niedersachsen.de/steuer/finanzaemter/)).
- **General advice:** AStA (employment law) — international@asta-hannover.de
