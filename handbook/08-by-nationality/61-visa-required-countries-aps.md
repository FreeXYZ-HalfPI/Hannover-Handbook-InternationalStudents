## 1. Purpose

Most international students at LUH come from countries whose citizens need a **national visa (type D)** to study in Germany. This page explains the general procedure, country-specific extras such as the **APS certificate**, and how to cope with long waiting times.

## 2. Who needs this?

- Citizens of all countries **not** listed in [59](59-eu-eea-swiss.md) or [60](60-visa-free-nationalities.md) — for example most countries in Africa, the Middle East, South and Central Asia, South-East Asia and many Latin American countries. Check with the [Federal Foreign Office – Visa requirements](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/-/231148).
- 🌍 **APS countries:** applicants whose school or university education was in **China, India or Vietnam**.

## 3. Steps

**General procedure**

1.  **Find your responsible mission** (the embassy or consulate for your place of residence) and read its information sheet ("Merkblatt") on student visas ([Federal Foreign Office – German missions abroad](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/ReiseUndSicherheit/deutsche-auslandsvertretungen/03-webseitenav)).
2.  **Book an appointment early.** Many missions use a service provider or the online **Consular Services Portal**, where you can upload your documents ([Consular Services Portal](https://digital.diplo.de/visa)). 🌍 In some countries waiting times are many months or there are waiting lists; book as soon as you have your admission (or as soon as the mission allows).
3.  **Typical documents:** application form, passport, biometric photos, admission letter, proof of finances (blocked account: €992/month in 2026), health insurance, CV and certificates, language certificate, and sometimes a motivation letter ([German Embassy San José – Student visa information (PDF)](https://san-jose.diplo.de/resource/blob/1540562/fddac503242f24102b78bf7523dd7aba/merkblatt-studium-data.pdf)). → [2. Before you arrive](../01-arriving-in-hannover/02-before-arrival-visa.md)
4.  **Visa fee:** usually €75 ([German Embassy San José – Student visa information (PDF)](https://san-jose.diplo.de/resource/blob/1540562/fddac503242f24102b78bf7523dd7aba/merkblatt-studium-data.pdf)).
5.  **Processing:** the mission may consult the Ausländerbehörde in Hannover; processing often takes several weeks. Apply only to the mission responsible for you — applications at other missions (e.g. in a neighbouring country) are usually rejected unless you legally reside there.
6.  **If your visa will be late:** tell the university in writing before the enrolment deadline. → [12. Admission and enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md)
7.  **After arrival:** register your address and apply for the residence permit **before the visa expires**. → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)
8.  **Rejected?** You can ask the mission to reconsider (remonstration) within the period stated in the rejection; the letter explains how. ⚠️ Free advice: AStA or the Refugee Law Clinic.

**APS certificate (China, India, Vietnam)**

1.  **What it is:** the Academic Evaluation Centre (Akademische Prüfstelle, APS) at the German embassy, run with the DAAD, checks the authenticity and plausibility of your school and university certificates before you apply to German universities and for your visa ([TUM – APS overview](https://www.tum.de/studium/bewerbung/infoportal-bewerbung/aps); [University of Saarland – APS](https://www.uni-saarland.de/studieren/international/infos/aps.html)).
2.  **When:** apply for APS **before** your university application — processing can take several weeks (interviews may be required, e.g. in China and Vietnam). Your APS certificate is usually needed for the uni-assist application and the visa.
3.  **Where:**
    - 🇨🇳 China: APS Beijing (and Shanghai) — [aps.org.cn](https://www.aps.org.cn/)
    - 🇮🇳 India: APS India, New Delhi — [aps-india.de](https://aps-india.de/)
    - 🇻🇳 Vietnam: APS at the German Embassy Hanoi — [vietnam.diplo.de](https://vietnam.diplo.de/) (section "Akademische Prüfstelle")
4.  ⚠️ Exceptions exist (e.g. for some scholarship holders or exchange students). Check your APS office's website.

**Other country-specific points**

- 🌍 **School certificates:** in many countries a school-leaving certificate alone does not give direct access to a German Bachelor's programme; you may need one or two years of university at home or the Studienkolleg ([anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/); [uni-assist – Country information](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/info-country-by-country/)). → [1. Applying](../01-arriving-in-hannover/01-application-uni-assist.md)
- 🌍 **Document legalisation:** some missions require certificates to be legalised or verified before accepting them. → [11. Family](../01-arriving-in-hannover/11-family-reunification.md)
- 🌍 **Sanctions or banking restrictions** can make paying for a blocked account difficult. → [62. Sanctions and payment restrictions](62-sanctions-payment-restrictions.md)

## 5. Responsible office

- **Visa:** the German mission responsible for your place of residence
- **APS:** the APS office in your country (see above)
- **University help with visa questions:** Welcome Centre → [22. International Office](../03-university-services/22-international-office.md)
