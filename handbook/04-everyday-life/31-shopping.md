## 1. Purpose

A guide to everyday shopping and saving money in Hannover: student discounts, bottle deposit (Pfand), shop opening hours and where to find food from home.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** newcomers.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Student discounts:** show your enrolment certificate for discounts at theatres, the opera, cinemas, museums, adult-education courses and some companies ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
2.  **Hausmarke card:** a free discount card for Hannover students at more than 100 shops, restaurants, museums and clubs; get it from the **Tourist Information** office ([hannover.de – Hausmarke](https://www.hannover.de/Wirtschaft-Wissenschaft/Wissenschaft/Studieren-in-Hannover/Hausmarke)).
3.  **Theatre and opera:** LUH students can attend the Staatstheater Hannover with the "Theaterflatrate"; its share (Kulturticket) is included in the semester fee ([Studentenwerk – Save money](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/money/save-money)). → [34. Sports and culture](34-sports-culture.md)
4.  **ISIC card** for discounts outside Germany ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
5.  **Bottle deposit (Pfand):** single-use bottles and cans carry a **€0.25** deposit, reusable bottles usually €0.08 or €0.15. Any shop selling that type of packaging must take it back, even if bought elsewhere; shops under 200 m² only take back their own brands ([Federal Environment Ministry – Single-use and reusable](https://www.bundesumweltministerium.de/faqs/einweg-und-mehrweg); [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/umwelt-haushalt/abfall/fragen-und-antworten-alles-zum-einwegpfand-und-dosenpfand-11505)).
6.  **Sundays and public holidays:** shops in Lower Saxony are generally **closed**; only a few Sundays a year are allowed by the city ([IHK Braunschweig – NLöffVZG](https://www.ihk.de/braunschweig/branchen/handel/einzelhandel/durchfuehrung-verkaufsoffener-sonntage-4500236)). Do your weekend shopping on Saturday. ℹ️ Shops in the main station are an exception.
7.  **Discount supermarkets** such as Aldi, Lidl, Penny and Netto are usually cheapest. ℹ️ General experience.
8.  **Food from home:** Hannover has many international grocery stores (Turkish, Arabic, Persian, Asian, African, Eastern European, Latin American) and halal butchers, especially in districts such as Linden, Vahrenwald and around Steintor. ℹ️ Community tip — help us build a list on [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md). → [64. Religion, food and home communities](../08-by-nationality/64-religion-food-communities.md)
9.  **Second-hand:** furniture and household goods are cheap on classified sites, at flea markets and in social department stores (Sozialkaufhaus). Halls often have "give-away" shelves. ℹ️
10. **Studentenwerk's money-saving list:** [Save money](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/geld/geld-sparen) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
