## 1. Purpose

Know emergency numbers and urgent help **before** you need them. Save this page on your phone.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **Everyone.**

## 3. Steps

| Situation | Number | Notes |
|----|----|----|
| Fire, ambulance, danger to life | **112** | European emergency number; free, 24/7 ([hannover.de – Emergency services](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/B%C3%BCrger-Service/Hilfe,-Beratung,-Notdienste/Notdienste-Notfall%C2%ADnummern)) |
| Police | **110** | Free ([hannover.de – Emergency services](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/B%C3%BCrger-Service/Hilfe,-Beratung,-Notdienste/Notdienste-Notfall%C2%ADnummern)) |
| On-call doctor (non-urgent, nights and weekends) | **116117** | [116117.de](https://www.116117.de/) |
| Emotional crisis | **0800 111 0 111** · **0800 111 0 222** · **116 123** | TelefonSeelsorge; free, 24/7 and anonymous; chat and e-mail at online.telefonseelsorge.de ([TelefonSeelsorge](https://www.telefonseelsorge.de/telefon/)) |
| Violence against women | **116 016** | Free, 24/7, in 18 languages ([Hilfetelefon Gewalt gegen Frauen](https://www.hilfetelefon.de/en.html)) |
| On-duty pharmacy | Online search at aponet.de | [aponet](https://www.aponet.de/notdienstsuche) |
| Emergency on campus | LUH emergency information | [LUH – Emergency](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/notfall) |

1.  **Calling 112:** say **where** you are, **what** happened, **who** is calling and **how many** people are injured; then wait for questions ([BBK – First aid and emergency call](https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Vorsorge/Gesundheit-und-Hygiene/Erste-Hilfe-und-Notruf/erste-hilfe-und-notruf_node.html); [hannover.de – Emergency services](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/B%C3%BCrger-Service/Hilfe,-Beratung,-Notdienste/Notdienste-Notfall%C2%ADnummern)). Operators usually also speak English.
2.  If you cannot speak, the official **nora** app allows a silent emergency call ([BBK](https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Vorsorge/Gesundheit-und-Hygiene/Erste-Hilfe-und-Notruf/erste-hilfe-und-notruf_node.html)).
3.  **Warning apps:** NINA (federal warning app) sends alerts about fire, floods, severe weather and other dangers for your location ([BBK – NINA](https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Warn-App-NINA/warn-app-nina_node.html)). The nationwide **warning day (Warntag)** is usually the second Thursday in September: a test alert at 11:00 and the all-clear around 11:45. Next Warntag: 9 September 2027 ([BBK – Nationwide warning day](https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Bundesweiter-Warntag/bundesweiter-warntag_node.html)).
4.  **Lost passport or residence card:** inform the Ausländerbehörde immediately and report theft to the police. 🌍 Only **your own country's embassy or consulate** can issue a new passport (EU citizens can also get emergency help from any EU country's embassy if theirs is not reachable); the Ausländerbehörde issues the new residence card, which can take 4–8 weeks ([City of Munich – Loss of residence documents](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/verlust-von-aufenthalts-oder-ausweisdokumenten/10112606/); [City of Wiesbaden](https://www.wiesbaden.de/vv/produkte/33/Verlust-Aufenthaltstitel-Reiseausweis)). ℹ️ Ask the Hannover Ausländerbehörde for its exact procedure. → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)
5.  🌍 **Your embassy or consulate:** find your country's diplomatic mission in Germany in the Federal Foreign Office's list ([Federal Foreign Office – Missions of other states in Germany](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/reiseundsicherheit/vertretungen-anderer-staaten)). Register with your embassy if it offers a registration service for citizens abroad. → [64. Religion, food and home communities](../08-by-nationality/64-religion-food-communities.md)
6.  **Discrimination or harassment at university** → [27. ChancenVielfalt](../03-university-services/27-chancenvielfalt.md)
7.  **Victims of crime:** the WEISSER RING helps victims free of charge ([WEISSER RING](https://weisser-ring.de/english)).
8.  **World War II bombs (Kampfmittel):** old bombs are still found in Hannover, and thousands of people may have to leave their homes for a few hours. Check the evacuation area and emergency shelters on the [Feuerwehr Hannover](https://www.feuerwehr-hannover.de/) website, and take medicines and important documents with you.
9.  **Heat waves:** follow heat and severe-weather warnings at [DWD – Warnings](https://www.dwd.de/DE/wetter/warnungen/warnWetter_node.html) or in NINA. Drink enough water and avoid going out at midday.
10. **Fraud, theft and free legal help** → [67. Consumer rights, scams and free legal help](../07-resources/67-consumer-rights-legal-help.md)
