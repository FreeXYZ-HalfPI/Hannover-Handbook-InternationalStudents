## 1. Purpose

Groups and programmes that connect international students with each other and with German students — including associations for students from a particular country or region.

## 2. Who needs this?

- International students and anyone interested in cultural exchange.
- 🌍 Students who want to meet people from their own country or language community.

## 3. Steps

1.  **List of registered student groups** (cultural, international, religious, political, subject-related): [Student associations](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/studentische-vereinigungen) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). 🌍 Students from many countries and regions have formed their own associations — search the list for your country, region or language, or ask AStA.
2.  **AStA's international department:** supports international student groups and the foreign students' commission (AusländerInnenkommission) and organises intercultural events ([AStA](https://www.asta-hannover.de/angebote-beratung/internationale-studierende)). → [24. AStA](../03-university-services/24-asta.md)
3.  **Erasmus Student Network (ESN):** organises events and trips for exchange and international students in many university cities ([ESN – Find a section](https://esn.org/sections)). ⚠️ Check whether a Hannover section is currently active.
4.  **Study-Buddy:** the International Office's intercultural exchange programme ([Study-Buddy](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/international/internationale-studierende/betreuungsangebote/study-buddy)). → [48. Programmes for international students](48-international-programmes-luh.md)
5.  **Language tandem** → [35. Learning German](../04-everyday-life/35-learning-german.md)
6.  **Founding your own group:** student groups can register with the university and get support from AStA (rooms, funding for events) ([LUH – Student associations](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/campusleben-und-engagement/gruppen-selbstverwaltung/studentische-vereinigungen)).
7.  **Communities outside the university** (cultural associations, religious communities, consulates) → [64. Religion, food and home communities](../08-by-nationality/64-religion-food-communities.md)
8.  ℹ️ Know an active group that is missing? Add it via [page 57](../07-resources/57-contribute-experiences.md).
