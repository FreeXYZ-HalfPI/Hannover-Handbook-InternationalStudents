# 54. Checklist — Before Arrival, First Week, First Month and First Semester

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 7 — Resources & Appendices](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

An ordered checklist of essential tasks from before departure to the end of your first semester; each item links to its full page. Based on the [university's Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist) and the pages of this handbook.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** newcomers. Items marked **(non-EU)** apply only to students without EU/EEA citizenship; items marked **(visa)** apply to visa-required nationalities only.

## 3. Checklist

**Before you travel**

- [ ] Find out which rules apply to your nationality → [58. Which rules apply to you?](../08-by-nationality/58-overview-which-rules-apply.md)
- [ ] Apply by the deadline and get a VPD from uni-assist (non-EU) → [1. Applying](../01-arriving-in-hannover/01-application-uni-assist.md)
- [ ] APS certificate (China, India, Vietnam) → [61. Visa-required countries & APS](../08-by-nationality/61-visa-required-countries-aps.md)
- [ ] If your family is coming: requirements and timing → [11. Family](../01-arriving-in-hannover/11-family-reunification.md)
- [ ] Book a visa appointment and get a national type D visa (visa) → [2. Before you arrive](../01-arriving-in-hannover/02-before-arrival-visa.md)
- [ ] Blocked account or other proof of finances (non-EU) → [2. Before you arrive](../01-arriving-in-hannover/02-before-arrival-visa.md)
- [ ] Travel or health insurance; EHIC for EU students → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)
- [ ] Search and book accommodation → [4. Accommodation](../01-arriving-in-hannover/04-housing.md)
- [ ] Pay the semester fee and upload your photo → [12. Enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md), [13. Semester fee](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
- [ ] Sign up for Study-Buddy → [48. Programmes for international students](../06-student-community/48-international-programmes-luh.md)

**First week**

- [ ] Get the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung from your landlord → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)
- [ ] Book and attend the Anmeldung (within two weeks of moving in) → [5. Registering your address](../01-arriving-in-hannover/05-anmeldung.md)
- [ ] SIM card → [8. SIM card and internet](../01-arriving-in-hannover/08-sim-internet.md)
- [ ] Join statutory health insurance and have the M10 sent → [7. Health insurance](../01-arriving-in-hannover/07-health-insurance.md)
- [ ] Complete enrolment → [12. Enrolment](../02-starting-your-studies/12-admission-enrolment.md)

**First month**

- [ ] Open a bank account → [6. Bank account](../01-arriving-in-hannover/06-bank-account.md)
- [ ] Apply for a residence permit before your visa expires / within 90 days (non-EU, and Swiss) → [10. Residence permit](../01-arriving-in-hannover/10-residence-permit.md)
- [ ] Activate IdM, Wi-Fi and Stud.IP → [17. Online systems](../02-starting-your-studies/17-online-systems.md)
- [ ] Get your semester ticket → [30. Public transport](../04-everyday-life/30-public-transport.md)
- [ ] Activate your TIB library card → [14. Campus and buildings](../02-starting-your-studies/14-campus-buildings.md)
- [ ] Attend O-Woche and Orientation Days → [16. Programme structure](../02-starting-your-studies/16-study-structure.md), [51. Events](../06-student-community/51-events-networking.md)
- [ ] Download your examination regulations and plan your timetable → [16. Programme structure](../02-starting-your-studies/16-study-structure.md)
- [ ] Register for language or sports courses → [26. Language Centre](../03-university-services/26-language-centre.md), [34. Sports and culture](../04-everyday-life/34-sports-culture.md)
- [ ] Save the emergency numbers and find your embassy/consulate → [37. Emergency numbers](../04-everyday-life/37-emergency.md)

**First semester**

- [ ] Register for exams in time → [18. Exams](../02-starting-your-studies/18-exams.md)
- [ ] Follow up on your Steuer-ID if it hasn't arrived after three months → [9. Tax ID](../01-arriving-in-hannover/09-tax-id-broadcasting-fee.md)
- [ ] Register for the Rundfunkbeitrag (once per dwelling) → [9. Tax ID](../01-arriving-in-hannover/09-tax-id-broadcasting-fee.md)
- [ ] Pay next semester's fee (Rückmeldung) → [13. Semester fee](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
- [ ] Re-validate your LeibnizCard for the second semester → [13. Semester fee](../02-starting-your-studies/13-semester-fee-leibnizcard.md)
- [ ] Convert your driving licence within six months if needed (non-EU) → [38. Driving licence](../04-everyday-life/38-driving-licence.md)

**End of studies**

- [ ] Apply for a job-seeker permit or Blue Card before your study permit expires (non-EU) → [46. After graduation](../05-work-finance-career/46-after-graduation.md)
- [ ] Deregister your address, Rundfunkbeitrag and contracts when leaving Germany → [47. Leaving Germany](../05-work-finance-career/47-leaving-germany.md)
