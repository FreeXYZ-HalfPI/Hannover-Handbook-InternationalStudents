# 15. Faculties, Institutes and Departments — Fakultäten & Institute

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 2 — Starting Your Studies](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

For subject advice, exams and regulations you need to know which **faculty (Fakultät)** and **institute (Institut)** your programme belongs to. If you don't know your faculty, ask the Central Student Advisory Service ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students.

## 3. Steps

1.  **The nine faculties** of LUH ([LUH – Faculties](https://www.uni-hannover.de/universitaet/fakultaeten)):
    - Fakultät für Architektur und Landschaft — Architecture and Landscape Sciences
    - Fakultät für Bauingenieurwesen und Geodäsie — Civil Engineering and Geodetic Science
    - Fakultät für Elektrotechnik und Informatik — Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
    - Fakultät für Maschinenbau — Mechanical Engineering
    - Fakultät für Mathematik und Physik — Mathematics and Physics
    - Naturwissenschaftliche Fakultät — Natural Sciences
    - Juristische Fakultät — Law
    - Philosophische Fakultät — Humanities
    - Wirtschaftswissenschaftliche Fakultät — Economics and Management
2.  Besides the faculties there are three **schools**: QUEST Leibniz Research School, Leibniz School of Education and Leibniz School of Optics and Photonics ([LUH – Research in the faculties](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/forschung/wissenschaft)).
3.  On your faculty's website, find the **subject advisor (Fachstudienberatung)** and the **programme coordinator (Studiengangskoordination)** ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). Many English-taught Master's programmes have a dedicated coordinator for international students.
4.  **Institutes** are smaller units within a faculty that run courses and research; you usually contact them for student assistant jobs (HiWi) and your thesis. ℹ️ General explanation. → [40. Student jobs](../05-work-finance-career/40-student-jobs.md)
