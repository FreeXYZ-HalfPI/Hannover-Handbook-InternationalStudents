# 16. Programme Structure, Choosing Courses and Your Timetable

> [!TIP]
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-24 · **Section:** [Section 2 — Starting Your Studies](README.md) · **Key:** ⚠️ = needs confirmation · ℹ️ = note · 🌍 = depends on your nationality

## 1. Purpose

In Germany you usually do not receive a ready-made timetable; you choose your courses yourself according to the **examination regulations (Prüfungsordnung)** and build your weekly schedule ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). For many international students this is the biggest difference from their home system.

## 2. Who needs this?

- **All** students, especially in the first semester.

## 3. Steps

1.  **Download and save** your programme's examination and internship regulations, as they may change ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). Where to find them: [Exam information & subject advice](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/pruefungsinfos-fachberatung).
2.  Check which modules are compulsory (Pflichtmodul) and which are electives (Wahlmodul), and how many **ECTS** credits each carries. One ECTS credit corresponds to about 25–30 hours of work; a full-time semester is about 30 ECTS. ℹ️ General explanation.
3.  **Course types:** Vorlesung (lecture), Übung (exercise/tutorial), Seminar (discussion, often with a presentation or paper), Praktikum/Labor (lab). Attendance is often not checked in lectures but is required in seminars and labs. ℹ️
4.  Find courses in the **online course catalogue**, published from 1 September (winter) and 1 March (summer): [Course catalogue](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/vorlesungen) ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
5.  Some courses require **registration in Stud.IP** ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [17. Online systems](17-online-systems.md)
6.  **"c.t." and "s.t.":** a lecture at "10 c.t." starts at 10:15 (academic quarter); "s.t." means sharp. ℹ️
7.  Your first-semester timetable is usually put together during **orientation week (O-Woche)** with help from senior students ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)). → [20. Fachschaft](20-fachschaft.md)
8.  **Preparatory courses (Vorkurse)**, especially in engineering and science, are optional but strongly recommended ([LUH – Study checklist](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/im-studium/start-ins-studium/study-checklist)).
9.  **Recognition of previous credits:** if you studied elsewhere, ask your examinations office about crediting modules (Anerkennung) early. ⚠️ Rules vary by programme.
